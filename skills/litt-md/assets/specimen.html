Rules

The ten signature rules. Each one is checkable; the critique workflow scores against them.

  1. P1
    Light, not paint

    The accent shows up as light. At most one flat accent fill per view.

  2. P2
    One light source

    Light comes from the top. Highlights on top edges, glows never contradict.

  3. P3
    Glass is earned

    Only floating surfaces are glass. Cards in flow are solid.

  4. P4
    Depth without drop shadows

    Cards stay flat. Floating surfaces get a zero-offset glow and a 1px ring.

  5. P5
    Material is static

    Glow, grain and gradients never animate.

  6. P6
    Precision

    8px grid, 1px hairlines, tracking tightens with size.

  7. P7
    Airy rhythm

    One idea per section, 128/96/64 spacing, asymmetric by default.

  8. P8
    Designed, not inverted

    Each theme has its own material values.

  9. P9
    One weight

    Everything at 400. Hierarchy comes from size, color and tracking.

  10. P10
    Roomy controls

    Buttons and inputs share 32 / 44 / 56. Padding equals the air around the label.
