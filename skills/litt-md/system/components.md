Their motion behavior is specified below and built in the gallery (`gallery/` in this repo). ## Button
`--radius-control`. Three sizes on the shared control scale (R9); the label is wrapped in `.litt-trim` so the padding equals the visible air at sm and md. lg runs wider than its air, so a short label never makes a square block:

| Size | Height | Side padding | Label | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sm | `--control-sm` 32 | `--control-pad-sm` 12 | 13 | Table rows, toolbars, dense UI |
| md | `--control-md` 44 | `--control-pad-md` 16 | 14 | Default |
| lg | `--control-lg` 56 | `--button-pad-lg` 32 | 16 | Hero CTAs only. A page form's submit and Cancel are md, like a dialog's actions. |

The label is at `--weight-title` at every size, with no extra tracking. Hover changes the fill and color on the instant tier (`--dur-instant`, `--ease-out`). A press sinks the button `--press-depth` (half a pixel, R4), and a secondary button's cast tightens at once (`--block-press`). On coarse screens a pressed button also takes its hover fill, so the press shows under reduced motion, which keeps it from sinking. The box never resizes. Grouped buttons sit `--control-group-gap` (12) apart.

| Variant | Fill | Text | Edge | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | `--accent` (hover `--accent-hover`) | `--accent-fg` | `--glow` plus a soft top highlight | The one main action per view (R6) |
| Secondary | `--block` (hover the block a step toward `--fg`) | `--fg` | The raised block (R3): `--block-lift`, tighter `--block-press` while pressed | Everything else important |
| Ghost | Transparent (hover: fg at 6%) | `--fg-muted` → `--fg` | None | Tertiary actions, toolbars, cancel |
| Danger | `--block`, as Secondary; in a dialog's footer `--danger` (hover a step toward `--fg`) | `--danger`; in a dialog's footer `--danger-fg` | The raised block; in a dialog's footer the primary's top highlight and no glow | A destructive action (R7). The red fill is only the confirm of the dialog that asks, scoped to `[data-dialog-footer]`, and it lies flat while disabled, until the dialog's condition is met |
| Disabled | `--surface-2` | `--fg-disabled` | Inset 1px `--line`, so it keeps its shape on a surface-2 area | `cursor: not-allowed`; keep it in the tab order only if it explains itself |

- **Loading:** keep the width, swap the label for a 16px spinner (stroke `currentColor`, 1.5), and set `aria-busy="true"`.
- **Icons:** 16px, 1.5px stroke, `--control-gap` (8) from the label. An icon may also come as a child next to the label, as shadcn writes it: each run of text is a trimmed label and each element sits in the row. Icon-only buttons are square in size (32/44/56), round (`--radius-pill`), and need an `aria-label`.
- **No arrow suffix by default.** An arrow is allowed only when the button navigates away from the site.

## Input
`--radius-control`, on the same control scale as buttons: `--control-md` (44, padding 16, 14px text) by default, in dialogs and in stacked forms; `--control-lg` (56, padding 24, 16px) only when it shares a row with an lg button (the inline form); `--control-sm` (32) in tables and toolbars. The field is the cut (R3): `--cut` inside the inset `--line-control` line, with its top wall's shade (`--cut-shade`): a floor a hair into the page, white in light and a step below the surfaces in dark. Text `--fg`, placeholder `--fg-subtle`. The line keeps the field at 3:1; the shade only says the floor sits lower. Never the tray's grey floor, which reads as disabled.
- **Inline form:** an lg input and an lg primary button side by side (`1fr auto`, `--space-2` apart), stacking full width below 640px.
- **Focus:** the line turns `--focus`, the shade stays, plus `--focus-ring`. Remove the default outline only when you replace it this way.
- **Error:** the line turns `--danger` and the shade stays; set `aria-invalid="true"`, and add help text in `--danger` linked with `aria-describedby`. The message says how to fix it.
- **Label:** above the field, `--type-small-size` at `--weight-title` in `--fg`, `--space-2` gap. The typed text stays at `--weight`. Placeholders are never labels.
- **Help text:** `--type-small-size` in `--fg-subtle`, `--space-2` below.
- **Disabled:** it lies flat, as nothing can be typed: `--surface-2` with the plain `--line` hairline and no shade, the text `--fg-disabled`.
- **Textarea:** the same field for longer text, with the same edge, focus, error, label and help text. It has no fixed height, so its size sets the air above and below the text, and it resizes vertically only.

## Number field
A number someone sets: seats on a plan, a limit, a price. The Input's field with − and + inside its end, a label above and a hint or an error below.
- **Field:** the Input's look, sizes and states (sm 32, md 44, lg 56; sm grows to 44 on coarse pointers). The number at `--weight` with tabular figures, so it never shifts as it changes, and an optional unit after it in `--fg-subtle`.
- **Steppers:** raised blocks in the field's end (R3: you press them), − then +, set 4px in from the field's edge and 4px apart: 24, 36 or 48px square (36 for sm on coarse pointers), `--block` with `--block-lift` at the control radius less 2px. Pressed, the cast tightens (`--block-press`) and the block sinks `--press-depth`. At its bound a stepper lies flat: transparent, its glyph `--fg-disabled`. A press steps once; held, it repeats after 400ms, every 70ms. They are not Tab stops and leave focus where it was, so a phone's keyboard never opens under a thumb that is only stepping.
- **Typing:** free while focused; read on leaving the field or Enter, in the page's locale, then kept between min and max, put on the step and shown formatted (`Intl.NumberFormat`, a currency or a percent). Text that is not a number goes back to the last value.
- **Keys:** ↑ ↓ step, Shift or Page Up and Page Down ten steps, Home and End the bounds.
- **Accessibility:** a spinbutton named by the label, with its value, bounds and the formatted value with its unit as `aria-valuetext`; the steppers are named "Increase seats".
- **Motion:** none. The edge fades on the instant tier, as the Input's.

## Code input
A short one-time code, such as the six digits from a sign-in email. It looks like one box per character and behaves like one text field.
- **Build:** one real `<input>` lies over the cells, invisible, with `autocomplete="one-time-code"`, so paste, the browser's autofill from a text message, password managers and screen readers all treat it as one field with its label. The cells are drawn from its value and hidden from assistive tech.
- **Anatomy:** the Input's field per cell (the cut: `--cut`, the inset `--line-control` line, `--cut-shade`, at `--radius-control`), square on the control scale (md 44, lg 56), each character in the sans at the body size, with tabular figures. In a box too narrow for them the cells share its width, never below 32px, so the page never scrolls sideways. Six cells split three and three with a wider gap. The label above at `--weight-title`, a hint or an error below, as a TextField.
- **Focus:** the cell the next character goes into takes the focus edge and ring, and an empty one shows a still 1px caret. Nothing blinks.
- **Typing:** only what the code can hold gets in (digits, or letters and digits in capitals), so a pasted "123 456" lands as 123456. The last character runs the completion callback once; the caller checks the code there. Backspace clears from the end.
- **Error and disabled:** an error puts the `--danger` edge on every cell and says what to do under them. Disabled lies flat: `--surface-2` with the `--line` hairline and `--fg-disabled`. The edges fade on the instant tier.

## Token input
A field that collects short values as removable tokens: sender domains, recipients, tags. The Input's sibling, with the Tag inside.
- **Field:** the Input's: minimum heights `--control-sm`, `--control-md` (default) and `--control-lg`, `--radius-control`, the cut (R3): `--cut` inside the inset `--line-control` line, with its top wall's shade (`--cut-shade`), the danger edge when invalid and the focus ring around the whole field while its text input is focus-visible. It grows by rows of 24px tokens, 4px apart; the text input takes the rest of the line, at least 8ch.
- **Tokens:** the raised Tag (`raised`: `--block` with `--block-lift`, the control radius less 2px, the label in `--fg`), a value you can lift out of the field, in the sans at the label weight; in a disabled field they lie flat, with a 16px remove button named "Remove @stripe.com" whose target grows to 44px on coarse pointers. `collapseAfter` folds the rest into a "+N" token on `--surface-2` (named "N more") until the field is focused.
- **Adding and removing:** Enter, a comma or leaving the field adds what was typed; a paste splits on commas, semicolons and line breaks; duplicates and empties are ignored. A rejected value stays in the input with its message under the field. Backspace in an empty input removes the last token. The remove buttons are one roving stop: left and right walk them, right past the last returns to the input, and Backspace or Delete removes the focused one. `max` stops adding and says so.
- **Accessibility:** a real label; tokens are a list named by it; a polite live region says "Added stripe.com" and "Removed stripe.com"; the hint, the rejection and the limit are linked descriptions. `name` posts one hidden input per value.
- **Motion:** a new token fades in with a 0.98 scale on the responsive tier, its neighbors slide by transform, and a removed one fades out on the exit.

## File drop
Where someone adds files, by dropping or choosing them, and watches them upload.
- **Area:** the cut at `--radius-surface` (`--cut` inside the `--line-control` line, with `--cut-shade`): a place you put files into, as a field is one you type into. A row, not a centered stack: a raised 32px square with an up arrow, the words ("Drop files here", then the types and the limit in `--fg-subtle`) and a secondary sm button, Choose files. While files are over it the line turns `--focus` and the words say what a drop will do, on the instant tier. On a touch screen it says "Add files".
- **Choosing:** the button opens the picker and is the only tab stop; a click anywhere on the area opens it too. Dragging is never the only way.
- **Checking:** a file of the wrong type or over the limit is turned away with one sentence under the area, in `--danger`, naming the file and why, in an alert. The rest are handed over.
- **Rows:** one per file, the name at the caption size, the size at the end, a Progress rail under the name while it uploads. A failed file keeps its rail where it stopped and says why, with Retry; Remove (or Cancel while it uploads) is a ghost icon button named for the file.
- **Disabled:** it lies flat, `--surface-2` with the `--line` hairline, no drop and no tab stop. The words stay `--fg-subtle`, because they still say something (when it opens again), as a disabled Slide to confirm's do.

## Select
One value from a short list (a role, a region, a plan). It replaces the native `<select>`, whose list cannot be styled on the desktop and whose rows are 28 to 30px on a phone.
- **Trigger:** a raised block, the secondary Button's look, since you press it (R3): `--block` with `--block-lift`, a hover a step toward `--fg`, and while pressed `--block-press` and the `--press-depth` sink; it stays pressed while its list is open. Sizes sm 32, md 44 (the default, in dialogs and stacked forms), lg 56, at `--radius-control`, 16px text on coarse pointers. Focus is the 2px `--focus` outline at a 2px offset. An error draws a `--danger` line under the lift, kept while pressed or open. Disabled lies flat (`--surface-2`, a `--line` hairline, `--fg-disabled`). The value is `--fg`, the placeholder `--fg-subtle`, and a 16px chevron in `--fg-subtle` sits at the end, `--control-gap` from the text. The chevron never turns.
- **List:** anchored below the trigger and flipped above it when there is no room, at least as wide as the trigger. It is the Menu's panel: glass at `--radius-surface` with the 4px inset, 44px rows at `--radius-control` with 12px side padding, and one gliding highlight. The current option carries a 16px check at the row's end in `--fg`: never bold, never a dot, never the accent. A long list scrolls inside the panel, which never grows past the room on screen.
- **Motion:** the responsive tier, as the Menu: the list grows from the trigger and travels a quarter of `--travel` from the side it opens on, and leaves on the exit token.
- **Keyboard:** Space, Enter or ↓ opens it on the current option; ↑↓ move, typing jumps to a match, Enter picks, Esc closes and keeps the value. Focus returns to the trigger.
- **Label:** above the trigger, as the Input's, with help text or an error under it. A form sends the value, through a hidden native field.
- **On compact screens** the list stays anchored, like a dropdown menu: a phone form's trigger is full width, so the list is too, and its rows are already 44px. It is not a sheet.
- Inside a dialog, a sheet or the header the list is solid (`--surface-2` and `--shadow-float`), never glass on glass.

## Combobox
Type to find one value, or several, in a long list: a client, a framework, a teammate. Select is for a short fixed list; Command menu is for actions.
- **Field:** the Input's look and sizes (sm 32, md 44 by default, lg 56), `--radius-control`, the cut (R3): `--cut` inside the inset `--line-control` line, with its top wall's shade (`--cut-shade`), the same focus, error and disabled states, 16px text on coarse pointers. The text is the query. A 16px chevron button in `--fg-subtle` at the end opens and closes the list; it never turns. A label above it, help text or an error under it, as the Input's.
- **List:** anchored under the field and flipped above it when there is no room, as wide as the field. The Menu's panel: glass at `--radius-surface` with the 4px inset, 44px rows at `--radius-control`, one gliding highlight, the picked option's 16px check in a fixed cell at the row's start so labels never shift. Optional group labels, as the Menu's. At most eight rows show, then it scrolls inside. With no match a quiet row says "No matches for “marl”" and nothing is lit.
- **Behavior:** the combobox and listbox pattern: focus stays in the field and `aria-activedescendant` names the lit option. Typing opens the list and filters it in the original order, ignoring case and accents, and lights the first match. With an empty query the picked option is lit. ↓ opens or moves, ↑ moves, both wrap and skip disabled options; Enter picks the lit option; Esc closes the list first, then puts back the picked option's label. Leaving the field closes the list and puts the label back, so the field never holds text that is not a value.
- **Several values (`multiple`):** the picks sit inside the field as Token input's raised tokens, before the query, each with its remove button. A pick clears the query and keeps the list open. Backspace in an empty query removes the last token.
- **Motion:** the responsive tier, as the Menu: the list grows from the field and travels a quarter of `--travel` from the side it opens on, and leaves on the exit token. Typing changes the list at once; its height is never animated.
- **On compact screens** the list stays anchored, never a sheet, because a sheet would cover the field. Its height is the visible screen below the field less `--space-2`, read from the visual viewport, so no option hides behind the keyboard.
- **Accessibility:** a real label; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and `aria-autocomplete="list"` on the field; a polite live region says how many options match a moment after typing stops ("4 options"). A form gets one hidden field per value with `name`.
- Inside a dialog, a sheet or the header the list is solid (`--surface-2` and `--shadow-float`), never glass on glass.

## Date picker
A day, or a range of days, from a month calendar: a launch date, a report's period. `Calendar` is the grid on its own; `DatePicker` is a field that opens it.
- **Values:** `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, never `Date` objects, so no time zone moves a day. `min`, `max` and `isDisabled` rule days out. The locale defaults to `en-GB` and the week to Monday, both props.
- **Calendar:** a header with the month and year at the body size in `--fg` ("October 2026") and previous and next as icon-only ghost sm buttons (pills). Weekday names in caption `--fg-subtle`, from `Intl`. Always six weeks, so the height never changes. Days are 40px cells (44px on coarse pointers) at `--radius-control`, numbers at the small size, tabular, `--fg`; days of the next and last month are `--fg-disabled`, and so are disabled days, which take no hover.
- **Day states:** hover fills with fg 6% on the instant tier. Today has an inset 1px `--line-strong` ring. The picked day is an `--fg` fill with `--canvas` text, never the accent (R6). In a range the two ends are filled and the days between them sit on an fg 8% band that runs square across the row and rounds only at its ends. While the second end is being chosen, hover previews the band.
- **Keyboard:** the date grid pattern: one day is a tab stop (the picked day, else today); ←→ move a day, ↑↓ a week, Home and End go to the week's ends, PageUp and PageDown a month, with Shift a year; Enter or Space picks. Focus is an inset 2px `--focus` ring.
- **Motion:** a new month slides half of `--travel` in from the side it comes from and cross-fades, on the responsive tier, as Tabs panels do; the month name cross-fades with it. Nothing else moves.
- **Date picker field:** the Select trigger's look and sizes, showing the date in words from `Intl` ("17 Oct 2026"; a range is "3 Oct to 17 Oct 2026") or a placeholder in `--fg-subtle`, with a 16px calendar icon at the end. It opens the Calendar in a Popover, which is a bottom sheet on compact screens. Picking a day closes it and focus returns to the field; in a range it closes after the second day. Optional presets ("Last 7 days") sit in a column of 36px rows beside the calendar, and above it as a row of sm secondary buttons on compact screens.
- **Accessibility:** the grid is labelled by the month; each day reads in full ("Friday 17 October 2026, today, selected"). The field is a button with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and a real label. A form gets the value (or `start` and `end`) through hidden fields with `name`.

## Checkbox and radio
Native inputs at 16px with `accent-color: var(--fg)`. A drawn checkbox is a 20px cut at `--radius-control` (`--cut` inside the `--line-control` line, with `--cut-shade`), a place you put a mark into. Checked or mixed, it fills with `--fg`, flush, its top edge catching the light, with a `--canvas` mark. A drawn radio is the round cut; chosen, it fills with `--fg` and holds a bead, an 8px raised block (`--block`, `--block-lift`). Disabled, both lie flat, the bead too. An error turns the line `--danger`. Not the accent (R6): a form can hold many checked boxes, and the accent stays on the one primary action. The label is on the right, `--space-2` gap, `--type-small-size`. The whole label is clickable.

## Switch
An on or off setting that takes effect as a setting, such as an email when a build finishes. A choice that can be wrong, such as accepting terms, is a checkbox, so a switch has no error state.
- **Anatomy:** a block in a tray, the press material (material.md, 6). The tray is 92 by 32, sunken, at `--radius-control`: `--tray` with `--tray-shade`. The block fills half of it, 44 by 28, set 2px in, raised: `--block` with `--block-lift`. Its corners are the control radius less those 2px, so the two stay concentric at every shape (the pill makes it a capsule).
- **It says its state:** the block reads "Off" in `--fg-muted` at the start and "On" in `--fg` at the end, at the caption size and `--weight-title`, in sentence case. Position and word both change, so color is never the signal. The word is hidden from screen readers, which hear the state from `aria-checked`.
- **Tone:** neutral by default: the block stays `--block` when on. `tone="accent"` fills it with `--accent` and its word with `--accent-fg` when on. That is the view's one accent fill (R6), so it belongs to the one switch that is the view's main choice, such as whether a site is live; a settings list stays neutral.
- **Hand:** a press presses the block down (`--block-press`). A click, a tap, Space or Enter toggles it. Or take the block and drag it: it follows one to one, says the side it is over, and lands on the nearer side when let go. Dragging is never the only way.
- **Motion:** the block crosses on the responsive tier (bounce 0), by translate; its fill, word and cast change on the instant tier. The tray never moves. Hover deepens the tray's floor by fg 4%.
- **Disabled:** the tray goes flat to `--surface-2` with a `--line` edge, and the block lies flat in it (`--surface-2`, a `--line` edge, no cast), its word `--fg-disabled`, still on its side. The label dims to `--fg-disabled`, but the description stays `--fg-subtle`: it is often what says why the switch is off-limits, so it must stay readable. A checkbox's description does the same.
- **Label:** the label sits right of the switch, `--space-3` away, at the small size and `--weight-title`, centered on the tray, and the whole row toggles it. A description can sit under the label in `--fg-subtle`. In a settings list the label and description come first and the switch sits at the row's end (`align="end"`), so the switches line up in one column.
- **Touch:** every row is at least 44px tall. The tray owns a sideways drag and leaves vertical scrolling to the page.
- Build it on a `role="switch"` button with `aria-checked`. Space toggles it. A form sends it like a checkbox.

## Slider
A value on a continuous range, such as the default animation speed. Use it when the position matters more than the exact number; when the number matters, use an Input.
- **Anatomy:** the post (material.md, 6): a raised plate the control's height (44, md) at `--radius-control` (`--block`, `--plate-lift`), a slot cut into it 6px in from each end, 16px down and 12px tall (`--slot`, `--slot-shade`), the part of the slot the cap has pulled out a step darker (`--slot-fill`), and a cap riding in the slot, 8px wide and 36px tall, standing taller than the plate (`--cap`, `--cap-lift`), with a grip line down its face. Its center travels from 14px in at each end. Never the accent (R6): a form can hold many sliders.
- **Notches:** step marks under the slot at the values you pass (`marks`), and a longer, darker one at home, the value the cap comes back to, which is the `defaultValue` unless you pass `home`. Within about a step of home, the hand lets go there.
- **It says its value (R5):** at the end of the label row, at the small size in `--fg-muted` with tabular figures, and in `--fg` while the cap is held. `showValue={false}` leaves it out.
- **Labels:** the label above, as the Input's. Words for the two ends ("Slower", "Faster") sit under the plate's ends at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, and say which way is which.
- **Hand (R4):** a press on the cap holds it where it was taken, so it follows the finger 1:1, with no easing; a press on the slot sends the cap there on the responsive spring, and from there it follows. Held, it sinks `--press-depth` and its cast tightens (`--cap-press`), at once. Pushed fast, it leans into the move, at most 4°, and rights itself on the spring.
- **Motion:** the cap and the fill move by translate on rails as wide as the cap's travel, so only transforms move. A key or a press on the slot glides on the responsive spring; a drag follows at once. Reduced motion stills the glide and the lean.
- **Keyboard:** ←→ and ↑↓ step, Page Up and Page Down step by a tenth of the range, Home and End go to the ends. The focus ring is on the plate.
- **Touch:** the plate is 44px tall, and all of it is the hit area. A drag that starts on a slider never moves a sheet.
- **Disabled:** everything lies flat: the plate and the cap go to `--surface-2` with `--line` edges, the slot to `--surface-3`, the notches away. The label dims to `--fg-disabled`; the value and the end words stay `--fg-subtle`, because they are still read.
- **Range:** two caps in one slot with the fill between them, for a span such as builds that took between 20s and 80s in a table filter. A press takes the nearer cap. The focused cap draws the focus ring around itself, so you can tell which one the keys move. Each cap is named by the label and its end ("Build time, from" and "Build time, to"). The span shows at the end of the label row in `--fg-muted`, tabular, and reads "20s to 80s": words, never a dash. The caps never cross, and stay at least `minStepsBetweenThumbs` steps apart (1 by default). The pair sits in a group named by the label. The keys act on the focused cap: Home and End take it to its end of the slot, as far as the other cap allows. A form sends both values under `name`.

## Pad
Two values set at once on one surface, such as a reply's tone against its length. It is the Slider in two dimensions; use two Sliders when the values are independent.
- **Surface:** a well cut into the page (R3, the cut): `--cut` inside the `--line-control` line, with its top wall's shade (`--cut-shade`), at `--radius-surface`, and a static dot grid (`--fg` at about 14%, 1.5px dots on a 16px pitch). The grid is a coordinate system inside a control, never page texture, and it never moves. The four axis words sit inside the edges, y's maximum at the top, at the caption size, `--fg-subtle`, sentence case, on plates in the well's color so the grid breaks around them. y grows upward.
- **Thumb:** a round cap, 20px (`--cap`, `--cap-lift`: lit on its top edge, casting straight down); held, it takes `--cap-held` and `--cap-press` and sinks `--press-depth`. Never the accent, and it never resizes. Its center stays 14px from every edge so the focus ring fits inside. A 1px dashed `--line-strong` line crosses the pad through it each way. `defaultMark` draws a 20px dashed circle in `--fg-subtle` with "Default" beside it.
- **Tooltip:** the chart tooltip's look (solid `--surface-3`, `--shadow-tooltip`) says the value in words beside the thumb, kept inside the pad; above a finger on touch. It shows while dragging, hovering or keyboard-focused, and Esc hides it.
- **Keyboard:** one tab stop. Left and right move x, up and down move y, Shift makes it 10 steps, Page Up and Page Down move y 10 steps, Home and End take x to its ends.
- **Accessibility:** two visually hidden range inputs, one per axis, named by the axis label and described by the pad's label, its help text and the words at the axis' ends, both with the formatted value as their value text. The y input is out of the tab order but reachable by a screen reader.
- **Touch:** the whole pad is the target (`touch-action: none`, `data-no-drag`).
- **Motion:** none on position. Only the tooltip fades, on the responsive tier.

## Color picker

## Rule builder

- **Form:** with `name`, the whole rule is posted as one JSON string.

## Icons
One set, drawn for litt on one grid. Use it before reaching for another set, so a view never mixes two drawings.

## Carousel
A set people look through in order, such as a project's screens, or a strip of cards. Never for content people must see: most people never reach a later slide.
- **Scrolling** is the browser's: a track with scroll snap, each slide resting at the start edge, so a swipe, a trackpad and Shift and the wheel keep their own momentum. A mouse can drag it, and the release goes to the nearest slide in the way it was going. The scrollbar is hidden; the peek and the controls say there is more.
- **Never on its own:** no autoplay, no loop, no timer. The person always moves it, so there is nothing to pause (Foundations, Motion).
- **Slides per view:** one, two or three, with the next slide peeking 48px (96px for one slide in a wide box), or slides of their own width. The box decides by a container query, not the screen; a narrow box is always one slide and a peek. Slides sit 16px apart.
- **Controls:** under the track, the range in words at the start ("3 of 12", "4 to 6 of 12"), at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, tabular, as Pagination's; round secondary sm icon buttons for Previous and Next at the end, 44px on coarse pointers. Next brings the first slide not wholly in view to the start; Previous goes a page back. At an end the button is disabled and hands its focus to the other one.
- **Keys:** the track is a tab stop with an inset focus ring; ← and → move one slide (swapped right to left), Home and End go to the ends.
- **Motion:** a button, a key or a drag's release scrolls smoothly; under reduced motion it jumps. No fades at the edges: material is static (R2).
- **Accessibility:** a region named by its label with `aria-roledescription="carousel"`, each slide a group with `aria-roledescription="slide"` named "3 of 12". Slides out of view stay readable. After a button or a key the new range is said once in a polite status.

## Callout
An inline note in the page flow: a tip, a heads-up, a result to act on.
- **Surface:** `.litt-raised`, padding `--space-4`, gap `--space-3`. In the flow, so never glass and no shadow, and never a left rail, a tinted fill or a colored border.
- **Anatomy:** a 16px icon on the title's first line, the title (body, `--weight-title`, `--fg`), a line of detail (small, `--fg-subtle`) 4px under it, an optional action at the end (an sm secondary Button or a Kbd) and, with `onDismiss`, a pill ghost close button named "Dismiss". It measures itself: under 28rem the action drops below the text.
- **Color:** only the icon takes `--success`, `--warning` or `--danger`; neutral is `--fg-subtle`. A non-neutral tone puts a hidden word before the title ("Warning: "), so the color never says it alone. A tip takes the neutral tone with its own icon.
- **Accessibility:** `role="note"`; `live` makes it a status, or an alert for danger, for a callout that appears after an action. Dismissing unmounts it, and the caller places focus.
- **Touch:** the close button and a Button action grow to 44px; a Kbd action hides, as key hints do on touch.
- **Motion:** none.

## Accordion
Sections that open in place: questions and answers, groups of settings, a long spec.
- **Anatomy:** rows split by 1px `--line` hairlines, with one above the first and one below the last. Each trigger is a full-width button at least 56px tall, its label at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, left-aligned, and a 16px chevron in `--fg-subtle` at the end that brightens to `--fg` on hover (instant tier, color only). The content sits under its trigger in `--fg-muted` at the small or body size, at most `--measure` wide, with `--space-6` below it. No card around it unless the page puts one there; no fills, no accent.
- **Behavior:** one open at a time by default (`type="single"`, and the open one can close), or several (`type="multiple"`). Triggers sit in headings (h3 unless told otherwise) and carry `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- **Keyboard:** Enter or Space toggles; ↑↓ move between triggers; Home and End go to the first and last.
- **Motion:** the panel's height is never animated: it snaps open. Its content fades in and drops a quarter of `--travel`, the chevron turns half a turn, and the rows below move to their new place by transform (Motion `layout`, position only), all on the responsive tier, so the page never jumps. Closing fades the content on the exit token while the rows below rise.

## Scroll area
A region that scrolls inside the page: a long list in a fixed-height card, a log, a side panel. Never the page itself.
- **Scrolling** stays native (wheel, touch, keyboard, momentum). Only the scrollbar is litt's: a 6px thumb at `--radius-control` in fg 24%, fg 40% while hovered or dragged, on a transparent track with 2px of inset, so the hit area is 10px. It overlays the content and shows while the area scrolls or the pointer is over it, then fades out on the instant tier after a short pause.
- **Edges:** a 1px `--line` hairline appears on any edge that has more content beyond it, and goes when that edge is reached (instant tier, color only). No gradient fades: material is static (R2).
- **Focus:** a scrolling region is a tab stop with an accessible name, so the arrow keys scroll it, with an inset 2px `--focus` ring.
- **Axes:** vertical by default, horizontal or both on request.
- **On coarse pointers** the thumb shows only while scrolling, because there is no hover.

## Tree
Things inside things, opened a level at a time: a project's files, a docs site's pages.
- **Rows:** 32px (44 on coarse pointers) at `--radius-control`, indented 16px a level. A 16px chevron for a branch, an icon (folder or file unless given), the label at the small size, truncated, and an optional detail in a tabular caption, `--fg-subtle`, at the end (a size, a change mark). A 1px `--line` guide runs down each level under the chevrons, so nesting reads without color.
- **States:** hover fills fg 6% on the instant tier. The selected row is raised (R3, selected is raised): the press material's block (`--block`, `--block-lift`) on whatever the tree sits on, its label in `--fg` at `--weight-title`. Never the accent.
- **Opening:** the chevron turns a quarter on the responsive tier; the children appear in place at once. No height animates, as in the Accordion.
- **Keys:** one Tab stop. ↑ ↓ through the rows that show, → opens or goes in, ← closes or goes out, Home and End, Enter and Space select (and toggle a branch), * opens the siblings, typing jumps to a name.
- **Accessibility:** the WAI-ARIA tree: `tree`, `treeitem` with level, set size, position, `aria-expanded` and `aria-selected`, children in a `group`.

## Resizable
Panels side by side or stacked, with a line between each two that a person drags: a tree beside a log, a list beside its detail.
- **Line:** a 1px `--line` hairline with a 12px hit area (44 on coarse pointers). `--line-strong` under the pointer and `--fg-subtle` while dragged, on the instant tier. No grip and no dots; the cursor says it moves. The focus ring when it has keyboard focus.
- **Behavior:** sizes are percents, each panel between its minimum and maximum. A handle moves only the two panels beside it, one to one with the pointer, with no easing or snapping. A double click puts the two back. ← → (↑ ↓ stacked) move it 5%, Shift 20%; Home and End give the panel before it its bounds. The layout can be kept in the browser.
- **On compact screens** two panels side by side are rarely both readable: show one at a time behind a Segmented control.
- **Accessibility:** each handle is a `separator` with its value (the size of the panel before it), its bounds and `aria-controls`, named for what it resizes.

## Glass header
Sticky, `--header-h`, `.litt-glass`, content in a `--container-wide` wrap.
- Order: wordmark (body size at `--weight-title`) → nav (small size in `--fg-subtle`; hover `--fg` with fg-6% fill) → right-aligned actions.
- **The current page is raised (R3):** the press material's block (`--block`, `--block-lift`) in `--fg`, as a switch's block and the selected segment are, and `aria-current="page"`. It keeps that look on hover. No dot, no underline, no accent, no weight change. In the menu on mobile, the current page carries a check.
- It is full-bleed, so it sets `border-radius: 0` (`.litt-glass` carries the surface radius).
- On mobile, hide the nav behind the command menu or a menu button. Don't squeeze it.
- **On compact screens** the nav stays behind the menu button and opens as the anchored dropdown (see Popover and dropdown). The bar stays sticky at the safe-area inset and never hides on scroll. Links and the menu button are `--control-md` on coarse screens.

## Breadcrumb
Where a page sits: Studio / Projects / Marlow Labs / Settings.
- **Anatomy:** a `nav` landmark named "Breadcrumb" holding an ordered list on one line. Each level is a link at the small size in `--fg-muted` that turns `--fg` on hover (instant tier, color only, no underline); the last is the current page, `--fg`, not a link, with `aria-current="page"`. Between levels a `/` in `--fg-subtle`, `--space-2` each side, hidden from screen readers. No dots, no chevrons, no accent.
- **Targets:** a link's hit area is 32px tall, and 44px on coarse pointers, without moving the text.
- **Long trails:** past four levels the middle ones fold into an icon-only ghost sm "…" button (a pill) named "Show 3 more levels", which opens a Menu of them. The current page truncates with an ellipsis rather than wrapping.
- **On compact screens** only the last two levels show; the rest fold into the "…" button.
- **Router:** links are plain `<a>` unless the caller passes its own link component.
- **Motion:** none.

## Steps
Where someone is in a flow of three to six steps, such as setting up a studio. Not for views someone moves between freely (Tabs) or a page's place in a hierarchy (Breadcrumb).
- **Marks:** the step marks of Plan and Tool call, so one mark means one thing everywhere: a check in a circle for done (`--fg-muted`), a half-filled circle for the current step (`--fg`, never spinning or pulsing), an empty circle for what is left (`--fg-subtle`). Never a number in a colored disc and never the accent.
- **Words:** the current label at `--weight-title` in `--fg`, done ones in `--fg-muted`, the rest in `--fg-subtle`, at the small size.
- **Row:** a hairline between steps, in `--fg` once the step before it is done. In a box narrower than about 640px it becomes "Step 2 of 4" and the current label, with one 4px rail per step, done and current in `--fg`. The box decides, by a container query, not the screen.
- **Column:** the hairline runs down between the marks, with a line under each step, for a side panel or a checklist.
- **Going back:** done steps may be buttons that go back. The current step and what is left never are: a flow moves forward by its own Continue.
- **Accessibility:** an ordered list named by the flow, `aria-current="step"` on the current one, each state in hidden text. Nothing animates.

## Tooltip
Solid, not glass, with `--shadow-tooltip`, `--radius-control` and small type in `--fg`.
- **Delay:** opens after a 200ms hover delay, and at once on focus.
- **Motion:** responsive tier. It grows from the trigger from `--press-scale` (0.98) and travels in a quarter of `--travel` from the side it renders on.
- **Exit:** `--dur-exit-responsive` with `--ease-out`.
- **On touch** a tooltip opens on a press and hold of 500ms, not on a tap, so a tap only does what the button does. (500ms is an interaction delay, like the 200ms hover delay, not a motion token.) It closes on the next touch anywhere. Shortcut chips are hidden on coarse screens. The label that matters must also be the button's `aria-label`, or visible text.

## Command menu
A native `<dialog>` in the top layer, `.litt-glass` with `--glass-shadow: var(--shadow-modal)`, `--radius-surface`, max width 560px, 15vh from the top.
- **Input:** 52px tall, 16px search icon in `--fg-subtle`, no visible border except a bottom `--glass-border`.
- **Rows:** small size, `--radius-control`, padding `--space-2`/`--space-3`, in a list padded `--space-1` so the corners stay concentric. The lit row gets an fg-7% fill and `--fg`, plus a `↵` kbd hint.
- **Behavior:** the combobox + listbox pattern (`aria-activedescendant`). The first enabled row is always lit. ArrowUp and ArrowDown move, wrap and skip disabled rows. Enter fires only on the lit row. Esc clears the query first, then closes. A backdrop click closes.
- **Filtering:** typing changes the list instantly, in the original order, and the panel never animates its height. The empty state says nothing matches and drops the Enter hint.
- **Motion:** responsive tier. It drops in over a dimmed canvas (`--dur-responsive`, `--ease-responsive`) with opacity, a quarter of `--travel` and `--press-scale`, and leaves on `--dur-exit-responsive`. One gliding highlight (transform, responsive) follows the lit row. The specimen shows the entrance and a per-row fill; the glide is built in the gallery.
- Open with ⌘K or Ctrl+K and a visible trigger button.
- **On compact screens** the panel sits at the top edge: `--space-2` below the safe-area inset and `--space-2` from each side, not 15vh down. Its list height is the visible screen less the keyboard, read from the visual viewport, so no row hides behind the keyboard.
- **Motion on compact** stays on the responsive tier: it drops from the top by half of `--travel`, scales from `--press-scale`, and leaves on `--dur-exit-responsive`. It is not draggable, because its list scrolls.
- **Touch:** the hints (↑↓, ↵, esc and row shortcuts) are hidden. A Cancel button, `--control-md` tall, sits beside the field. The field takes focus, because typing is the point.

## Popover and dropdown
Glass with `--shadow-float`, `--radius-surface`, padding `--space-1`, rows at `--radius-control` as in the command menu. Positioned in the top layer (`popover` attribute) or a portal.
- **Motion:** responsive tier. It grows from the trigger's corner from `--press-scale` (0.98) and slides a quarter of `--travel`. One gliding highlight (transform, responsive) follows the lit row. Exit on `--dur-exit-responsive`.
- **Open:** a pointer open lights nothing until the pointer moves. A keyboard open (Enter or ArrowDown) lights the first row with a focus ring.
- **Submenus** are solid, because glass never sits on glass.
- **Checkbox and radio rows** keep the check in a fixed 16px cell, so labels never shift, and the menu stays open while options change.
- **Edges:** near the bottom edge the menu flips up, and the entrance follows the side it opens on.
- **On compact screens** a popover becomes a bottom sheet (see Modal), because it holds fields and pickers that need the width and a place above the keyboard. A dropdown menu stays anchored: its rows are already 44px and the finger is beside it.
- **Submenus on compact screens** do not fly out sideways. The submenu replaces the list in the same panel, with a Back row first. The two lists cross-fade and slide as Tabs panels do: half of `--travel` in, a quarter out, on the responsive tier. The panel's height snaps to the new list and is never animated.
- **Touch rows** fill when pressed (instant tier, color only). Shortcut hints are hidden on coarse screens.

## Hover card
A preview of what a link points at: a person behind their name, a project behind its link.
- **Only a preview.** The trigger is a real link or button to the same thing. A touch screen has no hover, so a tap follows the link and the card never opens: nothing may live only in the card.
- **Open and close:** after the pointer rests 500ms on the trigger, or at once on keyboard focus (focus-visible only, so a tap never opens it). It closes 200ms after the pointer leaves both the trigger and the card, so the pointer can cross the gap, and at once on blur or Esc. Focus never moves into it.
- **Surface:** the Popover's: floating glass at `--radius-surface` with `--shadow-float`, padded `--space-4`, 256 or 320 wide; solid when its trigger sits inside glass.
- **Motion:** the Popover's, on the responsive tier: it grows from the trigger and travels a quarter of `--travel` from the side it opens on, and leaves on the exit token.

## Context menu
Actions on the thing under the pointer: right-click a row, a file, a card.
- **Look and rows:** the Menu's exactly: glass at `--radius-surface` with `--shadow-float`, 44px rows, the gliding highlight, checkbox and radio rows in a fixed 16px cell, solid submenus, labels and separators. The same row components work in both.
- **Open:** a right-click opens it at the pointer; Shift+F10 or the menu key opens it on the focused target; on touch, a press and hold of 500ms (the tooltip's hold) opens it at the finger and stops the browser's own callout and text selection on that target.
- **Motion:** the responsive tier. It grows from the pointer's corner and travels a quarter of `--travel` from the side it opens on after collision flipping, and leaves on the exit token.
- **Every action in it is also reachable another way** (a "⋯" Menu on the row, a toolbar, a shortcut). A context menu is a shortcut, never the only path, because a touch or keyboard user may never find it.
- **On compact screens** it stays anchored at the finger, like the Menu, and a submenu replaces the list in place with a Back row first.

## Modal
Glass panel at `--radius-surface` with `--glass-shadow: var(--shadow-modal)`, padding `--space-8`, max width 480px (forms) or 640px. The title is `--type-h4` (14px at `--weight-title`) in `--fg`, 4px above its description. Actions sit bottom right at md, primary last, secondary or ghost before it. The footer carries `data-dialog-footer`, so a danger Button in it is the view's one red fill: the confirm of a destructive step, flat while disabled until its condition is met (typing the project's name), never with a glow. Fields in a dialog are md too. The backdrop tint is `color-mix(in oklab, var(--canvas) 55%, transparent)`.
- **Motion:** the view's expressive moment: placed, not thrown. It rises by `--travel` and settles from `--press-scale` (0.98), and may bounce slightly (`--dur-expressive`, `--ease-expressive`). If the view has already used its expressive moment, the dialog uses the responsive tier (a `tier` choice on the component).
- **Exit:** `--dur-exit-expressive` with `--ease-out`. It never bounces.
- **On compact screens** it is a bottom sheet. `side="right"` becomes the same sheet, so no strip of page is left at the edge. It is glass, flush with the bottom, with `--radius-surface` on the top corners only, and it pads its footer by the safe-area inset. A 32px by 4px grabber in `--line-strong` sits at the top centre. The body scrolls inside, with `overscroll-behavior: contain`.
- **Motion on compact:** it always rises on the expressive spring and does not use up the view's expressive moment (Foundations section 6), whatever `tier` says. It rises by its own height, by transform only, and may bounce as the token allows (`--ease-expressive` peaks at 1.0012, about half a pixel on a 450px sheet). It leaves the same way on `--dur-exit-expressive` with `--ease-out`.
- **Above the keyboard:** the sheet's bottom follows the visual viewport, so the keyboard never covers its footer or the field being typed in.
- **Drag and buttons:** it can be dragged down from anywhere that follows the rules in Foundations section 6. The close button is 44px on coarse screens. Cancel, Esc and a scrim tap also close it.

## Lightbox
One image of a set at the size of the screen, opened from a Media.
- **Layer:** the whole screen, on the canvas at 97% that only fades (instant tier), so the image is the one surface and the page behind is a trace. No blur and no glass: the image is the material.
- **Image:** as large as the screen allows less the bars, at its own shape, in a Media frame (the surface radius, the hairline, `--surface-2` while it loads). Its width and height give it its shape before it loads.
- **Bars:** the label at the small size and `--weight-title` with "3 of 12" in a tabular caption beside it, and a round Close at the end, at the top. The caption under the image at the small size in `--fg-muted`, at most `--measure` wide. Previous and Next are round secondary buttons beside the image on a wide screen and at the end of the caption row on a compact one, 44px on coarse pointers, disabled at the ends. Nothing loops.
- **Motion:** opening is the view's expressive moment, as a dialog's: the image rises by `--travel` and settles from `--press-scale`, and leaves on the exit token; `tier="responsive"` when the view has spent it. The next image fades in from `--travel` on the side it comes from and the last one out by half that, on the responsive tier.
- **Touch:** a finger swipes the image sideways one to one (Foundations section 6). Past `--dismiss-distance` of its width or faster than `--dismiss-velocity` it carries on out and the next one comes in; pulled where there is nothing, it resists. The buttons stay.
- **Keys and focus:** focus lands on the panel so ← → Home and End work at once; Esc, Close or a click on the ground around the image closes it, and focus goes back to the image that opened it, or the slide it closed on.
- **On compact screens** it stays full screen; it is not a sheet, because it already fills the screen.

## Toast
Glass at `--radius-surface` with `--shadow-float`, padding `--space-3`/`--space-4`: the title at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, an optional description under it at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, at `--weight`. No status dot: an error's title is `--danger`, and the words say the rest. Bottom right on desktop, bottom center on mobile. `role="status"` (or `alert` for errors). Auto-dismiss after 5s, but never for errors. An undo action sits on the right as a ghost button or kbd hint.
- **Motion:** it arrives from just below its corner and settles. That is the expressive tier when the toast is the view's moment, otherwise the responsive tier. It leaves sideways by the same small distance, on the exit token for its tier.
- **Stacking:** older toasts stack behind the newest and open into a list on hover or focus.
- **On compact screens** the toast spans the page gutters, sits `--space-4` above the bottom edge or the safe-area inset, and leaves downward by `--travel` on a timeout, not sideways.
- **Over a sheet:** while a sheet is open on a compact screen, the toast docks at the top edge, `--space-2` below the safe-area inset (where the command menu sits), and arrives and leaves upward, so it never covers the sheet's buttons.
- **Swipe:** a sideways drag moves the toast one to one. It leaves past `--dismiss-distance` of its width or faster than `--dismiss-velocity`, in the direction it was going. The close button stays, 44px on coarse screens. A tap on the stack pins it open, as before.

## Table and list rows
Rows are separated by 1px `--line`, padding `--space-3`/`--space-4`, small size. The primary cell is `--fg`; the rest are `--fg-subtle`. Numbers and dates use the caption size at 400 with tabular figures, right-aligned. Hover is an fg-4% fill. A sticky header row is caption size at `--weight-label` in `--fg-subtle`, sentence case.
- **Hover:** the fg-4% fill is for airy lists (project rows, tables). Dense lists (menus, the command menu) use the gliding highlight. Hover never moves anything.
- **Entrance:** rows and raised cards arrive in one staggered cascade (`--stagger`, `--travel`, expressive tier). That cascade is the page's single expressive moment.
- **Filter and insert:** responsive and position-only (FLIP via transform), so text never stretches.
- **Selection:** a 20px box as the first column (the Checkbox, its label hidden and named for the row), a select-all box in the header that is mixed while only some rows shown are selected, and Shift for the range from the last box pressed. A selected row fills fg 6%, the press fill; the box carries the state. What to do with them goes in an Action bar after the table.
- **Filters:** a column's filter sits in its header, a 28px funnel button (44 under a finger) at the end of the header cell, before the label over numbers. It opens the Menu with the column's values: checkbox rows, any number on, each with how many rows it would show in the tabular hint, then Show all under a hairline; or radio rows with All first for buckets of a number. None on is no filter. Rows filter as they toggle; there is no Apply. While a column filters its button is raised (R3): the press material's block (`--block`, `--block-lift`), with how many values are on, in tabular figures. Never the accent. The count of rows shown is said in words above the table, in a polite status, with Clear filters beside it.
- **Wide tables:** the first one or two columns (the box and the row's name) can stick to the start edge while the table scrolls sideways, so a row never loses its label. They take an opaque fill that follows the row's hover and selection, and a 1px `--line` hairline at their edge once anything is scrolled under them. No shadow (R3).
- **Resizing columns:** a resizable header has a 12px handle at its end edge (20px under a finger) with a 1px hairline in `--line-strong` that shows under the pointer and on focus, `--fg-subtle` while dragged, on the instant tier. It follows the pointer one to one, never past the column's minimum; ← and → move it 16px (Shift 64), Home gives the minimum, a double click fits the column to its widest cell drawn. The first resize fixes every column at the width it has, so nothing jumps; from then a cell wider than its column ends in an ellipsis. The widths can be kept in the browser. The handle is a separator named for its column, valued in px.
- **Loading as you scroll:** rows too many to load at once come from the server a page at a time, asked for well before the end (twenty rows), so a steady scroll never waits. While a page loads, three skeleton rows wait after the last one, static like every skeleton, and the table says "Loading more rows" in its status. A page that fails is one row: what went wrong in words with the alert mark in `--danger`, and Retry. Never a spinner, and never a Load more button for a list someone is scrolling. Loading only goes forward; when people need to jump to a place or know the page they are on, use Pagination.
- **Filters in the address:** a table's filters, its search and its sort live in the page's query string, one parameter per value (`?status=failed&site=Halden+Gallery`), so a filtered view can be bookmarked, shared and reloaded, and Back brings the last one back. Writes replace the history entry, never add one, and wait a beat after typing. An empty filter leaves the address. Read it as untrusted: keep only values that are options. Selection, drafts and widths are not part of the view to share; they stay out of the address.
- **Big tables:** past a few hundred rows draw only the rows in view (a virtual body between two spacer rows), so ten thousand scroll as lightly as forty. Every row is then one line, the same height, and the table has a max height. It fixes the column widths the first rows give, so columns never shift as other rows scroll by; a wider cell ends in an ellipsis. It keeps its semantics: `aria-rowcount` on the table and `aria-rowindex` on each row. The entrance cascade and change motion are off: a long list arrives at once. Filter with a deferred query, so typing never waits on the rows, and give the table a search field, because the browser's find only sees the rows drawn.

## Action bar
What can be done to the rows or cards someone selected, while the selection lasts.
- **Place:** after the list, in the same scrolling area, sticky `--space-4` above the bottom of the view, centered; at the list's end it rests under it, so it never covers the last row.
- **Surface:** floating glass (it floats over content, R3) at `--radius-surface` with `--shadow-float`, padded `--space-1`. The count in words at the start ("3 selected", small, `--fg`), a hairline, the actions as sm buttons (secondary, or ghost for quieter ones), then a round ghost Clear. Never an accent fill; a destructive action says so in its word. Two or three actions; the rest behind a "More" Menu.
- **Motion:** it rises by `--travel` and fades in on the responsive tier, and drops by half that on the exit token. The count changes in place.
- **Keyboard:** a toolbar: one Tab stop, ← → between its buttons, Home and End, Esc clears. When it goes with focus inside, focus returns to where it came from.
- **Accessibility:** `role="toolbar"` named for what it acts on, and the count said once in a polite status as it changes.

## Pagination
Previous and next for a table or list that shows one page of many. The range is the main information, so it comes first, in words.
- **Range:** "21 to 40 of 143" at the caption size, `--fg-subtle`, tabular, at the start of the row. No dashes and no en dashes. With no rows it says "No results". It is a polite live region, so a screen reader hears the new range after a page change.
- **Buttons:** Previous and Next are `sm` secondary buttons, text only, no arrows, sitting `--control-group-gap` (12) apart at the end of the row. The first page disables Previous and the last disables Next. If the button that was just pressed becomes disabled, focus moves to the other one, so it never falls to the page.
- **Page buttons:** the numbers sit in one tray (`--tray`, `--tray-shade`, 2px in) between the two, 28px square (40px on coarse pointers), tabular, at weight 400; the current page is the raised block in it (`--block`, `--block-lift`, the control radius less 2px) with `aria-current="page"`, as the Segmented control's pick. Long runs fold into an ellipsis: first, last, and one either side of the current page. They only show when the row is at least 36rem wide (the row measures itself, not the window). Below that the range and the two buttons are enough.
- **Semantics:** a `nav` with its own `aria-label` ("Deploys pages"), and a list inside it. It sits `--space-4` under the table it pages.
- **Motion:** none of its own. The rows it pages are the table's job (Table, `animateChanges`).
- **On coarse pointers** Previous and Next are 44px tall and the page numbers 40px in their tray.

## Stat
A number tile for one figure that tells you how things stand: active projects, deploys this week, median build time.
- **Surface:** `.litt-raised` at `--radius-surface`, no shadow (R3), never glass (R3), never an accent rail. Padding is `--space-4` in a narrow group and `--space-6` from 48rem wide. The tile never has a hover, because it links nowhere.
- **Label:** above the value, small size, `--fg-subtle`, sentence case, no colon.
- **Value:** the h3 step (14px at `--weight-title`) in `--fg`, in the sans (not mono), with tabular numbers so a figure does not wobble when it updates. An optional unit ("s", "%") follows it directly, same size, in `--fg-subtle`.
- **Delta:** an optional line under the value at the caption size: a word and the number, "Up 12% on last week". The word says the direction. The color says whether it is news: `--success` for good, `--danger` for bad, `--fg-subtle` for neither. So a build time that fell is "Down 3 s on last week" in `--success`. Never an arrow glyph alone, never a dot. The good or bad judgment is also in hidden text ("good news"), so color is never the only signal.
- **Group:** a grid that measures itself. Two tiles across, four across from 40rem wide, one below 20rem. Tiles in a row share the row's height, and the gap is `--space-4`.
- **Semantics:** a description list. The label is the term and the value is its description, so a screen reader reads "Active projects, 12".
- **Trend:** an optional Sparkline at the foot of the tile (`trend`), under the delta and full width, so the trends in a row of tiles line up whether or not a tile has a delta. It reads after the value and the delta.
- **Motion:** none. A value that changes just changes. The entrance cascade belongs to the page.

## Details
An information card of labelled facts about one thing that people look up rather than compare: a deployment's domain and commit, an invoice's dates, a client's plan.
- **Surface:** `.litt-raised` at `--radius-surface`, no shadow (R3), never glass (R3), never an accent rail. `bare` drops the surface for a list that already sits on one, such as a dialog or a side panel. It links nowhere, so it has no hover.
- **Header:** an optional title (body size at `--weight-title`, `--fg`), a description (small, `--fg-subtle`) and one action at the end, an sm secondary Button on the title's line. A `--line` hairline separates it from the facts.
- **Rows (the default):** the label beside the value, rows split by `--line` hairlines, `--space-3` above and below so a line of text makes a 44px row. The labels share one column, as wide as the longest up to 40% of the card, so the values line up, and each label shares a baseline with its value's first line. Under 20rem wide (the card's own width) the label moves above the value.
- **Grid:** the label above the value, `--space-1` apart, in columns that measure the card: one under 20rem, two from there, up to four once it is wide. A long item such as a note takes the whole row.
- **Type (R8):** the label is small in `--fg-subtle`, the value body in `--fg`. Hashes, IDs and paths, which are code, may take Geist Mono at the small size (`mono`); versions, regions and everything else stay in the sans. A long value wraps; it never truncates.
- **Copy:** an icon-only ghost button at the end of the row copies the value exactly. It swaps to a check at once and a polite live region says "Domain copied". Always visible, 44px on a coarse pointer, and it never makes the row taller.
- **States:** a missing value says "Not set" in `--fg-subtle`, never a dash. Loading swaps each value for a still `--surface-2` bar one line tall, so the card keeps its height; no pulse.
- **Semantics:** a description list, so a screen reader reads "Region, fra1". A status in a value is a Tag. A link in a value is `--accent-text` with a soft underline.
- **Motion:** none. A value that changes just changes.

## Rolling number
A figure that updates in place while someone watches, because of something they did: a deploy count after Deploy, credits after a build. A Stat stays still ("a value that changes just changes"); a caller opts in by passing a Rolling number as its value.
- **Type:** the number only, in the font, size and color of where it sits, with tabular figures, formatted by `Intl.NumberFormat` (any locale and options: currency, percent).
- **Motion:** only the characters that change move. Each old one leaves half of `--travel` and fades, the new one comes in from the other side and fades in, on the responsive tier, inside a cell that clips them, as the Date picker's months do. Up from below when the value rises, down from above when it falls. Never further than the travel token, never a bounce, never the view's expressive moment. Characters line up from the right, so a new column and its separator simply appear. The first render does not roll; under reduced motion the digits simply change.
- **Accessibility:** the whole formatted figure is text for screen readers and the rolling cells are hidden. A change is not announced; the caller says it in a live region if it matters.
- Not for a figure that loads once, or one that changes many times a second (a timer).

## Chart
Bars compare amounts and a line follows change over time. Both are drawn in plain SVG, with no chart library, and both are quiet: R6 is the reason, because the accent belongs to the one primary action and a chart is made of many marks.
- **Color:** a mark is never the accent. By default it is the quiet foreground: one series is `--fg` at about 55% for bars and `--fg` for a line. The current mark (the last bar, or the last point) and the hovered or focused one are full `--fg`; while a mark is hovered or focused it replaces the current one, so only one is ever full. Up to three series step down the foreground: `--fg`, 55% and 30%, with a legend above the plot. At 30% a mark is under 3:1 against the card, so the third series' bars carry a 1px edge at 55% and its line is dashed.
- **Data hues (R7):** when telling series apart is the point, or a series has a color elsewhere in the view, it takes a data hue: `hue` on the series, or `hues` on the chart to give every series without one the next hue in the fixed order (blue, amber, teal, violet, rose). Color follows the series, never its place, so a filter never repaints the others. A hue series rests in the mark tone (3:1 on every surface) and a single series' emphasised mark takes the hue's `-text` tone, a step stronger; no edge and no dash are needed. Up to five hue series; a hue series and a foreground one can share a chart ("This week" in blue against "Last week" in the foreground). The legend and the tooltip still name every series.
- **Gridlines:** horizontal only, `--line` hairlines, with the zero baseline in `--line-strong`. Three to five lines in all. No vertical lines, no gradient, no area fill, no shadow, no glow on a mark.
- **Axis labels:** the caption size, `--fg-subtle`, tabular. Values sit at the start of their gridline, categories under their mark. When the marks are too close for their labels, every nth label shows, counting back from the last one so the current mark always keeps its label. Labels never overlap.
- **Bars:** at most 32px thick and at most 60% of their slot, so there is always air between them. Tops are square. A 2px corner is not on the radius scale, and the control radius would turn a 14px bar on a phone into a bullet. Grouped series sit side by side with a 2px gap. Bars grow up from the baseline.
- **Lines:** 2px, round joins, with a 4px marker on the current point and on the hovered or focused one. The points sit in the middle of their slots, so the first and last labels are never cut.
- **Tooltip:** a hovered or focused mark shows the Tooltip's look (solid `--surface-3`, `--shadow-tooltip`, `--radius-control`): the label in `--fg-muted` and the value in `--fg`, tabular, above the mark and kept inside the chart. It follows the pointer from mark to mark without re-entering. Esc hides it without moving focus. It repeats what the mark's label already says, so it is hidden from screen readers.
- **Marks:** the pointer target is the whole plot, and the mark under the pointer is the active one, so a thin bar is as easy to reach as a wide one. Hover changes color only, on the instant tier. A keyboard-focused mark gets a 2px `--focus` ring drawn around it, 2px away.
- **Motion:** one entrance on the responsive tier. Bars grow from the baseline by `scaleY`, and a line fades in by opacity. Height, width and path data never animate; new data redraws at once. Reduced motion is handled by the hooks.
- **Keyboard:** one tab stop, on the current mark. Left and right arrows move along the marks, Home and End go to the ends.
- **Accessibility:** the SVG is `role="img"` with a label that summarises the data ("Deploys per day, last 14 days: from 5 to 1, peak 9"), and a visually hidden table lists every value. A visually hidden button per category, named by its values ("3 Oct: 5 deploys"), sits in a labelled group outside the image: they are the keyboard's stops.
- **Touch:** a finger scrubs. The mark under it is active and shows its tooltip, which stays until the next touch elsewhere, and a vertical drag still scrolls the page. A column on a phone is narrower than a finger, which is why the target is the plot and not the column. The chart fills its container's width, the height is the caller's, and the plot never needs a horizontal scroll.
- **Stacked (growth step):** when each category is made of parts (storage added per day by kind), `StackedBarChart` stacks them into one rail per category: at most 8px wide, each segment at `--radius-control`, 2px of air between segments, like the Breakdown's split rail. A hue is a thread, never a block. Every series takes a data hue (its own `hue`, else the next in the fixed order), so a Legend can drop one without repainting the rest. The tallest stack sets the scale. The hovered or focused column takes its series' text tones over a faint column band, the tooltip lists each part and a Total under a hairline, and the hidden table adds a Total column. Up to five series.
- **Meter:** a horizontal progress bar for one value against a limit, such as storage used. The label sits at the start in the small size, the value at the end in tabular figures at the caption size, and the 4px rail under them is `--radius-control`, filled at fg 12% and then with `--fg` to the value, or with a data hue's mark tone when the color names the thing (images, video and fonts in a storage breakdown). `role="meter"` with a name and a value text. A `target` (a budget) is a 2px tick in `--fg-subtle`, 12px tall so it reads over the fill and the track alike, and is said after the reading. `steps` splits the rail into equal segments 2px apart for a count of a few (7 of 10 checks). The fill is never the accent. It does not animate.

## Range chart
One quantity over time as a barcode of thin bars, with a range you brush by hand and read as one figure: visitors per day across a launch, deploys per hour through an incident. A moment in a run is the Time scrubber, a value that stays is a Slider, and amounts to compare by height are a Bar chart. The figure is a sum, so the values must add up.
- **Anatomy:** the reading, the segment tray, the bars, the rail and the axis. The reading is the label at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, with a ghost "Whole range" button at the row's end that lies flat (disabled) while nothing is narrowed so the row never moves; the brushed total at the display size (18px, tabular) with the unit after it in `--fg-subtle`; and a line of words under it: "14 Apr to 11 May, 23% of the whole", or "All of 1 Jan to 31 Aug". The total is two inks: every group of digits but the last in `--fg`, the last group in `--fg-subtle`, because the last group is the one that churns while you brush and the quiet ink keeps the eye on the magnitude. Under 1,000 it is one group in `--fg`, and the group separator is the locale's, never a literal comma.
- **Segment tray:** a tray (R3: `--tray`, `--tray-shade`) the plot's width at `--control-sm` (44px on a finger), one button per segment as wide as its span, its label at the caption size and `--weight-title` and its share of the whole in tabular figures in `--fg-subtle` ("52%", "<1%"). The segment whose span equals the range is the raised block (`--block`, `--block-lift`, `aria-pressed`): selected is raised. The block glides between segments by transform on the responsive tier, as the Segmented control's does, and is gone when no segment matches. A segment too narrow for its label drops to its share, giving up its side padding before the share, and drops to nothing only under 32px; the accessible name keeps the words. Pressing a segment settles the range onto it.
- **Bars:** one 2px rect per drawn bar on a pitch of at least 4px. More bars than the width holds are drawn in equal groups by their mean, and only drawn: the hand, the keys, the figure and the words work in whole bars. A drawn bar is `--fg` once an edge has passed its centre and `--fg` at 55% otherwise, the chart family's two steps, its fill fading on the instant tier. Never the accent (R6), never a hue (R7: one quantity, the quiet foreground).
- **Rail:** a slot (R3: `--slot`, `--slot-shade`) cut under the bars, as wide as they are, holding the Range slider's anatomy: the fill between two raised caps (`--slot-fill`; `--cap`, `--cap-lift`, a `--cap-grip` line), 8 by 24px at the control radius less 2px, standing taller than the slot. Notches (`--notch`) under the slot mark each segment start: they are where a release is pulled to.
- **Drag (R4):** press on the bars or the rail. Within 8px of an edge (22px on a finger) it takes that edge, held where it was taken, following 1:1; inside the range it takes the whole span, which keeps its width; anywhere else a drag brushes a new range from the bar edge nearest the press. A press without a drag outside the range sends the nearer edge there. Edges never cross. A held cap sinks `--press-depth` and takes `--cap-press` at once. Let go and the edges settle from rest on the responsive tier onto the nearest bar, or a segment start within 8px, never past either end. `onValueChange` follows the hand bar by bar, and at once for a key, a press or a throw; `onValueCommit` fires once the edges have landed.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech:** three sliders in a group named by the label, "<label>, from", "<label>, to" and "<label>, move range", each with `aria-valuetext` in words ("14 Apr", and "14 Apr to 11 May" for the span). ← and → move one bar (Shift: ten), Page Up and Page Down go to the next and previous segment start (ten bars without segments), Home and End to the ends, Escape to the whole range; on "move range" the keys move the whole span. The segments are buttons named with their share. The svg is an image with a summary, a hidden table lists every bar with its value and its segment, and a polite status says the settled range and its total once, never while a hand still moves it.
- **Touch:** the plot owns sideways drags (`data-no-drag`, `touch-action: pan-y`), so the page still scrolls; the rail and the tray grow to 44px, an edge takes 22px of reach, and "Whole range" is md.
- **Disabled:** the slot `--surface-3`, the caps `--surface-2` with a `--line` edge, the fill fg 12%, the label `--fg-disabled`, the segments and "Whole range" disabled; no pointer, no tab stop. The figure and the words step to `--fg-subtle`, not `--fg-disabled`: they are still information.
- **Motion:** caps, fill and the drawn bars move by transform only; the figure rolls through the Rolling number and the tray's block glides on the responsive tier; fills fade on the instant tier. Under reduced motion the edges jump and the figure just changes. In fun mode (a trial) the span can be thrown: it coasts on the speed it was let go at, lands on the segment start nearest where it would stop (within 24px) on fun mode's throw spring, gives like a rubber band past either end, every bar an edge sweeps rises from its base once, and a held cap lifts in the hand. It rests exactly where it does without fun mode.

## Legend
Toggles which series a chart shows. The Legend filters and the chart draws: it holds which series are hidden, and the page passes the shown series to any chart, a table, or both.
- **Item:** a toggle button, `--control-sm` (32) tall with `--space-2` side padding at `--radius-control`: a 12 by 4px swatch rail in the hue's mark tone, the name (small, `--fg`), and an optional reading after it (caption at 400, `--fg-subtle`, tabular), such as a total. Hover fills fg 6%. On coarse pointers it is `--control-md` (44) tall and a press fills fg 6%, as rows do.
- **Hidden:** the swatch becomes the Meter's track (fg 12%) and the name `--fg-subtle`. Only color changes, on the instant tier: nothing moves, the weight stays 400, and the box keeps its size.
- **Hues (R7):** assigned exactly as the Chart's `hues`: a set hue is kept, and each item without one takes the next in the fixed order, counted over the whole list. A chart counts only the series it is given, so pin each shown series' hue (`legendHues`) and none repaints when another is hidden. Five items at most. Turn the chart's own legend off (`legend={false}`) so there is one.
- **The last one stays:** the last shown item cannot be hidden. It carries `aria-disabled` and a hidden note, "At least one series stays shown.", and is never natively disabled, so it keeps its tab stop.
- **Layout and keyboard:** a wrapping row, 4px apart, in a `role="group"` named by its label. Each item is its own tab stop with the house `:focus-visible` ring; Space or Enter toggles. Alt with a click or Enter shows only that item.
- **Motion:** none beyond the instant color change.

## Sparkline
A trend in one thin line, with no axes and no labels: median build time beside its figure, visits per day in a table row.
- **Anatomy:** a 2px line with round joins and caps, drawn at the container's width (the height is the caller's, 32 by default), and a dot on the latest point, 8px across over a 2px ring in the surface it sits on (`surface`). No area, no gradient, no glow.
- **Color:** the quiet default is the line at fg 55% and the dot in `--fg`. With a data hue the line takes the mark tone and the dot the `-text` tone, so today is the strongest mark. One hue means one quantity (R7).
- **Scale:** each line fits its own values with 2px of air. Fix `min` and `max` so a column of sparklines compares. `baseline` (a goal or an average) is a 1px dashed `--line-strong` line across the full width, and the scale includes it.
- **Accessibility:** `role="img"` with a summary ("Median build time, last 14 days: from 44 s to 41 s, low 37 s, high 46 s, baseline 45 s"). It shows shape, not values, so the exact figure always sits beside it: in a Stat, or a table cell. Not interactive: no tab stop, no tooltip.
- **In a Stat:** `trend` puts it at the foot of the tile, under the delta and full width, so the trends in a row of tiles line up.
- **Motion:** none. New data redraws at once.

## Breakdown
One whole split into its parts: storage by kind, credits by feature. The Meter, grown into a set.
- **Header:** the Meter's: the label at the start (small, `--fg`) and the reading at the end (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular: "91 of 100 GB").
- **Split rail:** 4px, a segment per part as wide as its share of the total, each at `--radius-control` with 2px of air between them, in the part's hue; when the total is more than the parts, the free space is one last segment at fg 12%. A part too small to draw keeps 4px, taken from the widest segment, so the rail never overflows.
- **Rows** (the default): one per part, `--space-6` apart, each exactly a Meter: the label, the reading with the share of the total in tabular figures ("24 GB  24%"), and a 4px rail on the fg-12% track filled in the part's hue. **Legend** (`layout="legend"`, for a tile): under the split rail, a wrapping row of entries, each a 12 by 4px swatch rail, the label and the reading.
- **Hues (R7):** parts take the fixed order unless the caller pins one, and a pinned hue is reserved so pinning never repaints the others. A hue is never used twice. Past five parts, the rest fold into Other at fg 55%.
- **Accessibility:** the split rail is an image with a summary naming the whole and every part ("Storage, 91 of 100 GB: Video 58 GB, Images 24 GB, Documents 6 GB, Fonts 3 GB, 9 GB free"); each row's rail is a meter named by its label, with its reading as the value text; the legend is a list.
- **Motion:** none. No hover, no focus stop.

## Bar list
A ranked list where each row carries a thin bar for its share: most-used shortcuts, busiest repos, top pages.
- **Anatomy:** an optional lead (a Kbd, a 16px icon or a small avatar, up to 48px so two key caps fit), the label (small, `--fg`, truncates), the bar, the value (small, `--fg`, tabular, at the end) and an optional delta. Rows are at least 44px, split by `--line` hairlines. One grid with subgrid rows keeps every bar starting and ending at the same place and the values in a column. The list measures itself: under 28rem the bar drops under the label at the row's full width.
- **Bar:** the Meter's rail: 4px at `--radius-control`, the track fg 12%, the fill `--fg` or one data hue's mark tone, as wide as the row's share of the largest value (or `max`). One hue means one thing: the quantity the bars measure, or, when the rows are kinds, each row's own hue (`hue` on the item), the one that kind keeps across the page.
- **Delta:** a word and a number at the caption size, colored and judged exactly as the Stat's: `--success` for good news, `--danger` for bad, `--fg-subtle` for neither, with the judgment in hidden text. Never an arrow alone.
- **Rows:** a row with `href` is a link, and with `onSelect` a button: an fg-4% hover fill at `--radius-control` on the instant tier, fg 6% pressed on coarse pointers, and an inset focus ring. A static row has no hover and no focus stop.
- **Accessibility:** an ordered list named by its label; a row reads "Archive, 412 times" with the delta's words. The lead and the bar are hidden: put anything that matters in the label.
- **Motion:** none. The bars never animate.

## Funnel
How many went on at each step: visited, signed up, activated, paid. Rows, not a trapezoid: the Meter, once per step, every rail measured against the first.
- **Header:** the Breakdown's: the label at the start (small, `--fg`) and the overall conversion at the end (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular: "4.1% overall", the last step as a share of the first). `overallText` replaces it.
- **Steps:** one row per step, in order, each exactly a Meter: the label, the reading with the step's share of the first in tabular figures ("6,930  38%"), and a 4px rail on the fg-12% track filled to that share. Shares are whole percentages, with one decimal under 10% so the small end still reads ("4.1%").
- **Between steps:** one quiet line at the caption size, weight 400, in `--fg-subtle`, that says how many of the step before went on, in words ("38% continued"). Never red, never an arrow or a dash doing the talking. `formatStep` rewrites it.
- **Hue (R7):** one data hue for the whole funnel, blue unless `hue` says otherwise. It is one quantity, so it takes one hue. Never the accent.
- **Bad data:** a step larger than the one before still draws, held at 100% of the first, and its line says "121% of the step before" instead of calling it a conversion. A first step of 0 draws empty rails and no shares.
- **Accessibility:** an ordered list named by the label. Each step's rail is a meter named by its label, from 0 to the first step's value, with the value and its share as its text ("6,930 people, 38% of 18,240"). The line between steps is plain text at the start of the next item, so it is read in order. The visible header and readings are hidden, and a visually hidden line says the overall ("Signups, 4.1% overall: 748 of 18,240").
- **Motion:** none. No hover, no focus stop.

## Gauge
One value against a range, on a half circle of fine ticks with the figure in its opening: credits left, storage used. A sibling of the Meter for when the number is the point.
- **Marks:** 48 radial ticks over 180 degrees, 2px wide with square ends. The ticks up to the value take a data hue's mark tone, or `--fg` without one (the quiet default); the rest are fg at 12%. The last filled tick marks the value: it reaches 4px further out, in the hue's `-text` tone or `--fg`. No needle, no glow, no gradient.
- **Opening:** the label above (caption, `--fg-subtle`), the figure in the sans at the h2 size in `--fg` with tabular numbers, and an optional caption below (caption, `--fg-subtle`). The text is HTML over the SVG, so it stays crisp while the drawing scales down to its container. Under about 200px leave the caption out.
- **Accessibility:** `role="meter"` named by its label, with a minimum, a maximum, a value and a value text; the caption describes it. The ticks and the text over them are hidden, so nothing is read twice.
- **Motion:** none, like the Meter: no entrance, no hover, no focus stop.

## Dot plot
A distribution as stacks of dots, so you see where most values fall without reading numbers: reply times, build times. Plain SVG, a sibling of the Chart.
- **Anatomy:** one stack per bin on an 8px pitch (a 6px dot and 2px of air), so the bin count follows the width, on a linear or log axis. Stacks grow out from a center line, mirrored. A dot stands for the fewest items that fit the tallest stack in the height, and the legend says so when it is more than one. No gridlines, no area, no glow.
- **Color (R7):** with a goal, dots at or under it take the hue's mark tone and dots past it `--fg-subtle`, split on an exact bin boundary. Without a goal every dot takes the hue. The goal is a 1px dashed `--line-strong` line labelled at its foot; the median a 2px line in the hue's `-text` tone, labelled "Median" in that tone. A marker is a 10px dot ringed 2px in `--fg` on the center line, named in the tooltip's look.
- **Type:** tick, goal and median labels at the caption size, tabular; ticks in `--fg-subtle`, and a label that would touch its neighbor is left out.
- **Legend:** a 6px dot, the name and the share in `--fg`, tabular. A key, never a status.
- **Interaction:** one pointer layer; the bin under the pointer turns to the hue's text tone (`--fg` past the goal) on the instant tier, with the chart's tooltip ("1 h to 2 h, 34 replies"). One tab stop, on the median's bin; arrows, Home and End move between non-empty bins, Esc hides the tooltip, and a focused bin gets a 2px `--focus` ring. A finger scrubs, with `touch-action: pan-y`.
- **Accessibility:** `role="img"` with a summary ("Reply times, 412 replies: median 1 h 5 m, 84% within the 4 h goal") and a hidden table of every bin, then the median, the goal and the markers.
- **Motion:** one fade on the responsive tier. Dots never squash or grow.

## Spread
How values spread, one thin rail per row on a shared scale: build times by site, reply times by client. The Dot plot's quiet sibling.
- **A row:** the label at the start (small, `--fg`, truncates), the rail, and the median at the end (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular). The rail: a 4px track at fg 12% across the scale, the full range over it at fg 24%, the middle half (the 25th to the 75th percentile) in the hue's mark tone, and the median as a 2 by 12px tick in the hue's `-text` tone. All at `--radius-control`. Rows are `--space-6` apart, with no dividers.
- **Alignment:** one grid, so every rail starts and ends at the same place and the medians line up. Under 28rem the label moves above its rail.
- **Scale:** linear or log, the ends rounded outward. The tick labels sit once under the last row, and a label that would touch its neighbor is left out. A goal is a 1px dashed `--line-strong` line through every rail, labelled once under the rows. The hue means the measure, so a row past the goal keeps it and says so in words.
- **Legend:** one line in words, with swatches in the same tones: "Faint: the full range. Blue: the middle half. Tick: the median."
- **Accessibility:** a list named by its label; each row reads "Halo site: median 41 s, middle half 32 s to 48 s, range 22 s to 71 s", with ", past the 4 h goal" when it is. The rails are hidden.
- **Motion and keyboard:** none; nothing is pressable.

## Heatmap
A dot matrix whose depth says how much: usage per day, deploys per week, mail per hour.
- **Anatomy:** circles on a pitch of the dot plus 4px (an 8px dot on 12px by default). A row has a caption label in `--fg-subtle` at the start and an optional total at the end (small, `--fg`, tabular); optional ticks name chosen columns. Under the grid, at its end: "Less", the five levels, "More". Under a 16px pitch only every second row is labelled, and a screen reader still hears all of them.
- **Color (R7, magnitude):** zero is fg at 8%; the four steps are one hue's mark tone over transparent at 30, 55, 80 and 100%, starting at the quartiles of the non-zero values unless `steps` says otherwise. No data draws nothing.
- **Interaction:** hover rings a dot 1px in `--fg` and shows the chart's tooltip above it ("3 Oct, 14 deploys"). A `role="grid"` with one tab stop: arrows move in two dimensions, Home and End along a row, Ctrl with them to the corners, Esc hides the tooltip; the focused dot gets a 2px `--focus` ring, 2px away.
- **Fit:** dots keep their size. A grid wider than its box scrolls sideways inside it, starting at the latest column, with an 8px static fade on an edge that has more beyond it; labels and totals stay put. A finger scrubs while the grid fits and taps to pick while it scrolls.
- **Accessibility:** the grid is named by its label and described by a summary ("Deploys per day, 214 in all, most on 12 Oct with 31"); every dot is named by its row, its column and its value.
- **Motion:** none beyond the tooltip's responsive entrance.

## Cohort grid
Retention by signup week, as a real table: a row per cohort, a column per period since signup.
- **Columns:** the cohort (small, `--fg`, sticky at the start), its size (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular, end-aligned, headed "Users"), then a cell per period: an 8px dot just before the value (caption at 400, `--fg`, tabular). Every number keeps the width of the grid's longest, so the dots and the figures each line up. A low value is never set quieter: both inks pass 4.5:1.
- **Color (R7, magnitude):** the Heatmap's: zero is fg at 8%, the four steps are one hue's mark tone over transparent at 30, 55, 80 and 100%, starting at the quartiles of the non-zero values unless `steps` says otherwise. A period a cohort has not reached draws nothing. Under the table, at its end: "Less", the five levels, "More", hidden from screen readers.
- **Look:** the Table's: a hairline box at `--radius-surface` filled surface-1, 44px rows on 1px `--line`, the header at the caption size and `--weight-label` in `--fg-subtle`. Period cells are compact, about 64px. `average` adds a last row over a `--line-strong` hairline: "All cohorts" (every user who reached the period, so cohorts weigh by size), or "Average cohort" for counts (the plain mean of the cohorts that reached the period).
- **Fit:** the table keeps its width and scrolls sideways inside its box, so the page never does. The cohort column stays put on the box's surface, with a hairline at its edge once the periods pass under it. While it scrolls, the box is one tab stop, a group named by the caption.
- **Accessibility:** a native table with a hidden caption, column headers and a row header per cohort ("12 Oct, Week 3, 41%"). An empty cell reads "no data". No roving grid and no tooltip: every number is on screen.
- **Motion:** none. No hover, nothing to pick.

## Timeline
The steps of a run in order: when each started, how long it took and how it ended, grouped into phases. TimelineBar draws the whole run as a strip beside it.
- **Anatomy:** an ordered list in four columns: the start (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular), the rail (a 1px `--line-strong` line through 16px status icons), the label (small, `--fg`; skipped and pending in `--fg-subtle`) and the duration (caption, `--fg-subtle`, tabular; "skipped" in words). Rows are at least 32px. A phase is a heading row (small `--fg-subtle` label, tabular total) over a nested, labelled list; the rail breaks at phase headings. The list measures itself, so the start column narrows on a phone and labels wrap.
- **Status:** a check in a circle (`--fg-muted`), a half-filled circle for running (`--fg`, never spinning or pulsing), an empty circle for pending and a dashed one for skipped (`--fg-subtle`), an x in a circle for failed (`--danger`). Every status is also said in hidden text. No dots.
- **Drawn to scale (`proportional`):** a step's row is at least its seconds times `secondHeight` (4px) tall, and a long step's rail becomes a 4px bar at `--radius-control` in its phase's hue (R7: the hue says which phase), or fg 55% without one; a failed step's bar is `--danger`. With the last run's time, a ghost bar at fg 12% stands beside it with "was 31s" at its foot. Short steps keep the thin line.
- **Result:** `TimelineResult` closes the run: its label at the body size in `--success`, `--danger` or `--fg` with the tone in hidden text, and the total at the end, tabular.
- **Strip:** `TimelineBar`, 32px at `--radius-control`, segments sized by seconds, square and 2px apart, in their phase's hue; phase labels above ("Build · 24s") and the times at phase boundaries below, in a tabular caption. A hovered or touched segment lifts by an fg-16% wash on the instant tier and shows the chart's tooltip. It is an image with a summary ("Build 24 seconds, deploy 18 seconds, 42 seconds in all"), and its segments are not focusable, because it always sits beside a list or a table of the same steps. On coarse pointers its hit layer is 44px tall; a tap pins the tooltip and a drag scrubs.
- **Accessibility:** a step reads "00:02, Run build commands, done, 19 seconds".
- **Motion:** none in the list; a running step never moves. Only the strip's tooltip enters, on the responsive tier.

## Log view
The lines a build, a deploy or a job writes as it runs. The Code block's surface, for output.
- **Surface:** `--surface-2` with the inset `--line` hairline at `--radius-surface`, a 32px header with the label at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, actions and Copy, and the lines in mono at the small size, padded `--space-4`.
- **A line:** its number in a gutter (mono caption, `--fg-subtle`, never selected or copied), its time in one right-aligned column as wide as the longest (so the text stays in line as "+9.6s" becomes "+10.2s"), then the text: info in `--fg-muted`, debug in `--fg-subtle`, success in `--success`. A warning's text is `--warning` and an error's `--danger`, each with the alert mark in the gutter and a hidden "Warning:" or "Error:", so color is never alone.
- **Following:** at the bottom, new lines keep the view there. Scroll up to read and it stays; a secondary sm button at the bottom says how many lines came since and goes back (smoothly, at once under reduced motion).
- **Accessibility:** the scroller is a named region and a Tab stop; lines are an ordered list and are not read out one by one. Say how the run ended in a status of its own (a Progress, a Toast).
- **Motion:** none of its own.

## App shell
The frame around an app's pages: a sidebar for moving between them and a main area for the page.
- **Desktop:** a 240px sidebar on `--surface-1` with a `--line` hairline on its edge, and a main area that scrolls with the page. The sidebar column runs the full height of the page, so it reaches the bottom of a tall page. Its content is sticky and as tall as the window, so the wordmark and the links stay in view while the page scrolls.
- **Wordmark:** at the top of the sidebar, body size, `--weight-title`, `--fg`, in a row as tall as the glass header (`--header-h`).
- **Links:** 36px rows at the small size and `--radius-control`, with 12px side padding. The current page is raised (R3): the press material's block (`--block`, `--block-lift`) in `--fg`, and `aria-current="page"`. The others are `--fg-muted`, and hover fills them with fg at 6% and brightens the text, on the instant tier. No dots, no accent, no weight change. An optional 16px icon sits before the label. The sidebar holds one or more `nav` landmarks, each with its own name.
- **Main:** the page's own landmark, with the page gutter at the sides and `--space-8` above and below.
- **On compact screens** the sidebar is gone. A glass top bar, sticky at the safe-area inset like the header, shows the wordmark and an icon-only menu button, 44px, labelled "Open navigation", with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. It opens the nav as the bottom sheet (see Modal): the same links in 44px rows, a heading, a close button, drag to close. Picking a link closes the sheet. The sheet does not use up the view's expressive moment.
- **Motion:** none of its own. The sheet is the Modal's.

## Empty and loading states
- **Empty:** one sentence saying what will appear here and one action to make it appear. Left-aligned in the container. No illustration unless art direction (Q5) says so.
- **Loading:** the skeleton fills with `--surface-2` at the real content's dimensions, and it is static. No pulse and no shimmer: a loop is motion that is not a response to the user, and a sweeping gradient animates material (R2).
- **Error:** what went wrong, in plain words, plus how to fix it or retry. Status color on the text or icon only.

## Progress
Work that is happening now, such as an upload or an import, with a known end. A quantity against a limit that stands still is a Meter (Chart).
- **Anatomy:** the Meter's lockup: the label at the small size in `--fg`, the reading at the end in tabular figures at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, and a slot cut into the page (R3: what fills), 8px tall at `--radius-control`: the floor `--slot`, the done part in it in `--fg`, and the walls (`--slot-shade`) drawn over the fill, so the fill sits down in the channel. Never the accent and never a data hue. The fill's end is square, since a radius squashes under its scale.
- **Ending:** done fills the slot and the reading says Done; failed keeps the fill where it stopped in `--danger` (a status mark, R7) and says Failed, with a message under it that says what to do. The end is announced once in a polite status.
- **Not known yet:** an empty slot and the reading in words ("Waiting"). Nothing sweeps, pulses or shimmers; a wait with no end in sight is the loading state of the button that started it.
- **Motion:** the done part is a full-width band scaled from the left, a transform on the instant tier. Under reduced motion it jumps.
- **Accessibility:** `role="progressbar"`, named by the label, `aria-valuenow` while known and the reading as `aria-valuetext`.

## Forms
How the controls above make a page. One left-aligned column, never centered (R9).
- **Spacing:** fields sit `--space-6` (24) apart. Related controls share a fieldset with a legend at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, like a field's label. A section's title sits 4px above its description. Groups sit `--space-12` (48) apart.
- **Settings page:** sections, each a title (`--type-body-size` in `--fg`) with one line of description in `--fg-subtle`, then its fields. From 1024 up the title and description take the left third and the fields the rest; below that they stack. A `--line` hairline and `--space-12` of air separate sections. Save comes last, alone, lg and primary.
- **Sign-up or contact:** one narrow column (about 400 wide), md fields, a consent checkbox above the submit, and the submit lg, primary and full width (R9).
- **Optional fields:** mark the optional ones, not the required ones: "(optional)" in `--fg-subtle` after the label. No asterisks.
- **Errors:** validate on submit, then again as each field changes, so a fixed field clears itself. Each invalid field shows its error under it, in `--danger`, saying how to fix it. Above the form an error summary names every problem: a `.litt-raised` panel with an inset 1px `--danger` edge, a title in `--fg` ("Fix 2 fields to save") beside a 16px alert icon in `--danger`, then one link per error that moves focus to its field. The summary takes focus when a submit fails. A form of three fields or fewer skips the summary and focuses its first invalid field.
- **Actions:** in a page form, the submit comes first at the column's start, with Cancel (ghost) after it, both md. Below 640 they stack full width, submit on top. A dialog keeps its own order: bottom right, primary last, md. Never disable the submit to say a field is wrong; let the submit show the errors.
- **Saving:** the submit shows its loading state, then a toast confirms. A failed save keeps every value.

# Agents

A chat between a person and an agent, and what the agent shows while it works: its thinking, its plan, its tool calls and the code it writes. The same rules hold here as everywhere. The one accent fill is Send. Nothing shimmers, pulses or loops. A live state is said in words and a clock, never in a moving gradient. Parts of a turn are bare on the page; only the person's bubble, an approval and a code block are boxes.

## Conversation and message
A chat, read top to bottom, newest at the foot.
- **The agent's turn has no bubble.** Its words sit on the page at the body size in `--fg-muted` (body copy, as everywhere), with a measure of about 68ch. `MessageText` styles what a markdown renderer puts inside it: paragraphs `--space-3` apart, headings at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, lists with the marker in `--fg-subtle`, links in `--accent-text`, inline code in `--font-mono` on fg 6% at `--radius-control`, strong text in `--fg` at `--weight-title` (never bold, R8), emphasis in `--fg`, upright, and table headers at the caption size and the label weight. The answer is the content, and a box around long prose cramps it.
- **The person's turn is a bubble:** right-aligned, `.litt-raised` at `--radius-surface`, padding `--space-3` and `--space-4`, text at the body size in `--fg`, at most 80% of the column (85% on compact screens) and 32rem. No tail and no accent fill: the accent stays on Send. Files the person sent sit above the bubble as the Composer's chips.
- **Who is speaking:** the side says it. Each turn is an `article` with a visually hidden heading ("You", or the agent's name). An optional `name` shows above an agent's turn at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, for a thread with more than one agent or person, with an optional sm Avatar beside it. A name is never colored.
- **Spacing:** turns sit `--space-8` apart. The parts of one turn (thinking, a plan, tool calls, the answer, its actions) sit `--space-3` apart.
- **Actions:** under a finished agent turn, a row of sm ghost icon-only buttons with tooltips: Copy (it swaps to a check and a polite live region says "Copied"), Retry, and the caller's own. They always show under the latest turn; under older turns they show on hover and focus-within, and always on coarse pointers, where they grow to 44px. A hidden row keeps its height, so nothing shifts when it shows. There are none while the turn streams, and none on a turn that stopped, whose Retry is in its error line.
- **Streaming:** text appears as it arrives, with no fade per word, no blinking caret and no shimmer. The composer's Stop and the Thinking clock say the agent is working. The actions appear when the turn ends.
- **Error:** a turn that stopped says so on a line under its text: a 16px AlertIcon in `--danger`, the words in `--fg-muted` ("The answer stopped: the connection dropped."), and a Retry sm secondary button. Status color on the icon only.
- **Conversation:** a labelled region that scrolls on its own and holds the turns in a column up to 48rem wide, centered in it, so the composer under it can share the column. It keeps the latest line in view while content grows, unless the reader has scrolled up. Then a "Jump to latest" sm secondary button with an ArrowDown icon shows `--space-4` above the bottom, centered, and jumps without a smooth scroll (litt has no token for one). It is `aria-busy` while the agent answers, and a polite live region says when a turn starts and ends ("Litt is answering", "Litt answered", or "Litt stopped answering" after an error), never the words as they stream. When the person sends, the view follows to the foot again.
- **Empty:** before the first turn, the Empty state's rule: one sentence on what the agent can do, then the Composer's suggestions.
- **Motion:** a new turn arrives once on the responsive tier: opacity and half of `--travel` upward. Not the expressive tier, because a chat has many turns and the view's one moment stays free. Nothing moves while text streams. The Jump button fades in and out on the responsive tier.
- **On compact screens** the column takes the full width inside the page gutter, the bubble 85%.

## Composer
Where the person writes to the agent: a text field that grows, files, a toolbar and Send.
- **Surface:** a box at `--radius-surface`, the Input's cut (`--cut` inside the `--line-control` line, with `--cut-shade`). While its text field has focus, the edge is `--focus` plus `--focus-ring`, as the Input's. It holds a text area and, under it, a toolbar row of sm controls, padded `--space-2`.
- **Text:** the body size (16px on coarse pointers) in `--fg`, the placeholder in `--fg-subtle` ("Message Litt"), side padding as the Input's md. It starts one line tall and grows a line at a time (the height snaps; it is never animated) up to `maxRows` (8), then scrolls inside. A visually hidden label names it.
- **Keys:** with a mouse or a trackpad, Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line. On a touch screen, whose soft keyboard has no Shift, Return starts a new line and Send sends. `enterSends` chooses either way; with it off, for long writing, ⌘Enter or Ctrl+Enter sends. Enter during IME composition never sends, and while an answer streams Enter sends nothing, so the next message can be drafted. Esc stops a streaming answer while the field has focus.
- **Toolbar:** the caller's controls at the start (an Attach ghost icon-only button that opens the file picker, a model Select at sm), Send at the end.
- **Send:** an icon-only round primary button at sm (44px on coarse pointers) with an ArrowUp icon, named "Send". It is the view's one accent fill (R6). It is disabled, in the Button's disabled look (so not an accent fill), while the field is empty or the agent waits on an approval. While the agent answers it becomes Stop: a secondary icon-only button with a filled square, named "Stop". Send and an approval's Allow are never both enabled, so a view never holds two accent fills.
- **Files:** chips above the text, raised blocks while they can be removed (`--block`, `--block-lift`, `--radius-control`, 32px; a sent message's attachment and a disabled composer's chips lie flat): a FileIcon, the name at the caption size in `--fg`, cut at the end with an ellipsis, the size in `--fg-subtle`, and a remove button named "Remove brief.pdf" that grows to 44px on coarse pointers. A file still uploading shows its percent in tabular figures. A paste attaches files only when the clipboard holds files and no text. Removing a chip moves focus to the next chip, or to the field. Files dragged over the composer turn its edge `--focus` and show "Drop to attach" in place of the placeholder, on the instant tier.
- **Suggestions:** before the first turn, up to four prompts above the composer as secondary sm buttons (the sans, the control radius). A click puts the prompt in the field and focuses it, so the person can edit before sending.
- **Hint and limit:** an optional line under the composer at the caption size in `--fg-subtle` ("Litt can be wrong. Check what matters."). With `maxLength`, a tabular caption count shows in the toolbar once 80% is used, in `--danger` at the limit.
- **Disabled:** it lies flat, `--surface-2` with a `--line` edge, the text `--fg-disabled`, and the reason in the hint.
- **On compact screens** it sits at the foot of the chat with the safe-area inset under it, and the caller keeps it above the keyboard (`useVisibleScreen`). The toolbar's buttons grow to 44px.
- **Motion:** only instant color fades. Send and Stop swap at once. A file chip enters as a token does (the responsive tier, opacity and a 0.98 scale) and leaves on the exit token.

## Thinking
The agent's reasoning, as a quiet disclosure above its answer.
- **Row:** a button 32px tall (44px on coarse pointers) with no fill: a 16px chevron first, in the column where a tool call shows its status mark, then the label at the small size in `--fg-muted` and the clock at the caption size in `--fg-subtle` with tabular figures. While the agent thinks it reads "Thinking 4s"; when it stops, "Thought for 12s" ("1m 5s" past a minute, "Thought for a moment" under a second). Hover brightens the label to `--fg` on the instant tier.
- **No shimmer, no pulse, no spinner.** The live signal is the clock, which counts whole seconds. A sweeping gradient animates material (R2), and a loop is motion that is not a response to the person.
- **Body:** the reasoning at the small size in `--fg-subtle`, indented to the label, with no box, no rail and no tint. Paragraphs sit `--space-2` apart.
- **Open and closed:** it is open while the agent thinks, so the reader can watch it work, and closes itself when thinking ends, unless the reader toggled it. `open` and `onOpenChange` control it. The chevron turns a quarter on the instant tier.
- **Motion:** the body fades in and travels a quarter of `--travel` down on the responsive tier, and leaves on the exit token. Its height snaps and is never animated.
- **Accessibility:** the row is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. The ticking clock is hidden from screen readers; a polite live region says "Thinking" once at the start and "Thought for 12 seconds" at the end.

## Tool call
What the agent did: a row per call, a group that folds a run of them, and an approval that asks first.
- **Row:** at least 32px tall (44px on coarse pointers): the 16px `StatusIcon`, the action in words at the small size in `--fg-muted` ("Read", "Ran", "Searched"), the target in `--font-mono` at the small size in `--fg` ("src/app.tsx", "npm test"), cut at the end, and the time at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, tabular, at the row's end. A call with details shows a chevron in a 16px slot at the row's end and opens them, as Thinking does; the slot stays on rows without details, so a run's times line up.
- **Status:** the Timeline's marks from `StatusIcon`. Running is the half-filled circle in `--fg`, never spinning. Done is the check in `--fg-muted`. Failed is the x in `--danger`, with "Failed" in words before the time. Waiting is the empty circle in `--fg-subtle` with "Waiting for approval", and denied is the dashed circle with "Denied". Every status is also said in hidden text.
- **Details:** the input and the output, each under a caption-size label in `--fg-subtle`, in mono at the caption size in `--fg` on `--surface-2` with an inset `--line` at `--radius-surface`, padded `--space-3`. Past about 16rem they scroll inside, focusable and labelled ("Output of npm test"). Output stays `--fg` when the call failed: the status says it.
- **What it found:** `details` holds a part with its own surface, drawn as given after the input and output: a Log view for a command, a source list for a search, file changes for an edit. It is never put in the output's well, which would draw a well inside a well. `result` is a few words after the target at the small size in `--fg-subtle` ("4 sources", "+2 −2", "Exit 1"); it never shrinks, so the target is cut first and a folded group still reads what each call did.
- **Group:** `ToolCallGroup` folds a run of calls under one row with the caller's summary ("Read 3 files and ran the tests"), or "4 tool calls". Its chevron sits at the row's end. It is open while any call runs, and closes itself when they are all done unless the reader toggled it.
- **Approval:** `ToolApproval` asks before a call runs. A `.litt-raised` panel in the flow (never glass), padded `--space-4`: a title at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg` ("Run a command?"), the call in mono on `--surface-2` at `--radius-control`, one line in `--fg-subtle` on what it touches, then md actions: Allow (primary, the view's one accent fill, because Send is disabled while it waits) and Deny (ghost), with an optional checkbox to always allow. After a choice it becomes a tool row, "Allowed" (the check) or "Denied" (the dashed circle). It does not take focus when it appears, because the person may be reading; a polite live region says "Waiting for your approval: npm run deploy". When the choice was made from inside the panel, focus moves to the row, so it never falls to the page.
- **Motion:** rows arrive with their turn. A status mark swaps at once and colors fade on the instant tier. Details open as Thinking's body does.

## Sources
Where an answer came from: a numbered mark in the running text that opens its source and previews it, and the same sources as rows under the answer or inside the search call that found them. One `Source` (`{ id, title, url, site?, passage?, readAt?, kind? }`) feeds the mark's card, the list and a search's results, and `Sources` around the turn numbers them once, by position.
- **Mark:** `Citation`, a 16px chip on the line, at least 16px wide with 4px of side padding at `--radius-control`, the number at the caption size and the text weight, tabular, in `--fg-muted` on fg 6%, 4px after the word. It never grows the line box. It is flat (R3): a reference you follow, not an object you move, so it never rises, takes no underline and no accent. It is a link to the source in a new tab (`rel="noreferrer"`), as citations are everywhere, and the Hover card's trigger: after the pointer rests, or at once on keyboard focus, the card grows from it on the responsive tier. Its accessible name is "Source 2: <title>", described by the site and "opens in a new tab". A mark whose id the `Sources` does not hold, or a mark outside one, draws nothing and warns once in development, because a mark can stream in before its source. A source whose url is not http or https makes the mark a plain chip with no link.
- **Card:** `HoverCardContent` md, floating glass. The kind's icon (`GlobeIcon`, or `FileIcon` for a file) and the site at the small size in `--fg-muted`, the title (small, `--weight-title`, `--fg`, two lines; a file's title in the mono) as the link, the passage in quotes (small, `--fg-muted`, four lines, no rail and no tint) and the read words (caption, `--fg-subtle`: "Read 2 minutes ago", from the caller's `readAt` words; no component reads a clock). A mark's `quote` replaces the passage for that one mark, because one page can back two claims.
- **List:** `SourceList`, an `ol`, bare in the turn. A heading holds the label (small, `--weight-title`) and the count (caption, tabular); `hideLabel` keeps it for assistive tech. Each row is one link to the source in a new tab, at least 44px, two lines: the title (small, `--fg`, cut at the end) over the site and the read words (caption, `--fg-subtle`). A 16px lead column holds the number chip, the mark's look but not a control, where a Tool call row shows its mark; the row's fill hangs 8px into the gutter so its text keeps the turn's edge. With no `sources` it lists the turn's. With `sources` (what a search found, in a Tool call's `details`) a row the turn cites wears its number and an uncited one shows its kind's icon in `--fg-subtle` with hidden "Not cited".
- **The light (R4, R5):** rest on a mark and its row takes fg 6% and every other mark of that source takes `--fg` on fg 12%; rest on a row and its marks light. Color only, on the instant tier; nothing moves. Keyboard focus does the same. The pairing never depends on it: the number in the text is the number in the list, readable with color and motion hidden. `Sources` holds the light as two fields, the pointer's and the keyboard's, merged where they are read, and marks and rows subscribe by id, so pointing re-renders only the ids involved.
- **Boundary:** `parseSources` is the one place a Source is checked. A later duplicate id is dropped, an href is kept only for http and https (a model's `javascript:` url shows as text), the site is read from the host when missing, and the number is the place in `sources`. The model writes `[2](#cite-geist)`; `citationId` reads that href and `unlinkCitations` turns the links back into `[2]` for Copy, so the scheme lives in one file and a fragment never reaches a hash router.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech:** marks and rows are links in the tab order. Focus on a mark opens the card and lights its row; Esc closes the card. The list is named by its label, and a row reads its number, title, site, read words and "Opens in a new tab". The card is a preview: everything in it is on the row or on the page the link opens.
- **Motion:** only colors fade, on the instant tier. The card is the Hover card's. In fun mode (a trial) resting on a row pops its marks in reading order on the pop spring, a wave apart, and each transform is cleared when it lands, so the rest frame is Calm's.
- **On compact screens** and on a finger the card never opens; the number pairs the mark with its row. A mark's hit area grows to 44px tall and 24px wide (an `after:` box), because two marks sit 4px apart in a line: the named exception to R9's 44 in width that prose links take. Rows are 44px, and a pressed row takes its hover fill.
- **Forced colors:** the chip keeps a 1px CanvasText border, and a lit chip or row takes Highlight and HighlightText.

## Plan
An agent's steps for a task, ticked off as it works.
- **Header:** the label at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg` and the count at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, tabular ("3 of 7"), over a 4px rail in the Meter's look, the done share in `--fg`. Progress is not a category, so it takes no hue (R7).
- **Steps:** an ordered list, rows at least 32px tall, each the 16px `StatusIcon` and the label at the small size. Pending is `--fg-subtle`. Running, the current step, is `--fg` with the half-filled circle. Done is `--fg-muted` with the check, never struck through: the color steps it back. Failed takes the x in `--danger`, its label stays `--fg`, and "Failed" follows in words. Skipped is the dashed circle in `--fg-subtle` with "Skipped". A step can carry a detail line under it at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`.
- **Surface:** bare in a turn, like the turn's other parts. It can also sit in a raised panel beside the chat; the panel is the caller's.
- **Accessibility:** an `ol` named by the label, each step's status in hidden text, and `aria-current="step"` on the running step. A polite live region says "Step 4 of 7: Run the build" when the running step changes, "Step 4 of 7 failed: Run the build" when one fails, and "Plan done" when every step is done or skipped. It never speaks on the first render. The count is done steps over all steps, so a skipped step leaves it one short.
- **Motion:** none on the steps. A status swaps at once and colors fade on the instant tier. The rail's fill moves at once, as the Slider's range does. Steps added when the plan changes fade in on the responsive tier.

## Run card
One agent run as a card that says what it is making, where it is and how far it has got, and folds into a block you press to open again, such as "Creating your world" while the person keeps writing. Steps with no card around them are a Plan; one job filling up is Progress.
- **Anatomy, open:** a flat card in the flow (`.litt-raised` at `--radius-surface`, padded `--space-4`, never glass) that fills its container. The header: the title at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg` over the words at the small size in `--fg-muted`, then the ring, the figure at the body size in `--fg`, tabular and as wide as "100%", the caller's actions (ghost, sm) and Hide (ghost, sm, a chevron down, 44px on a finger). On a narrow card (a container query) the header is two rows: the title and the buttons, then the ring, the words and the figure. Under it "Activity" at the caption size in `--fg-subtle` over the steps, a bare Plan, and the preview beside them when the card is wide enough, under them when it is not.
- **Anatomy, folded:** the same box as the raised block (`--block`, `--block-lift`, `--radius-control`, md height), the width of its words and never wider than its container. It is one button holding the ring, the title over the words (the card's sizes on 16px lines, the title cut at the end), the figure and Expand with a chevron up. It sinks `--press-depth` and its cast tightens to `--block-press` while pressed, at once. On a phone the word Expand is for assistive tech only and the chevron stays.
- **Words:** "Step 2 of 3" while a step runs, "Step 2 failed" when one fails, "Done" when every step is done or skipped and one is done, "Waiting" when nothing runs. Never "2/3", never trailing dots.
- **Ring:** the Gauge's ticks, closed: 32 two-pixel ticks, 2.5px long, round a 32px ring from the top and clockwise, a band of marks that never reads as a loading spinner. Lit in `--fg` up to the figure and `--fg` at 12% after it; a failed run's lit ticks take `--danger`, a status mark, as the Plan's x does. No hue (R7). At least one tick is lit above zero, and the ring is full only at 100%. Decorative: the words and the figure say the same.
- **Figure:** `progress` from 0 to 1 as a whole percent, rounded down so it never says more than the run has done, else the steps done over all the steps. A finished run is 100%.
- **Fold (R4):** the moment is a press, on Hide or on the block. The material swaps at once, as a press does. The ring travels between its two seats (a shared `layoutId` on a text-free part), the title, the words and the figure glide by `layout="position"`, and the box glides by its corner only, so its size snaps and its radius and shadow never scale. All on the responsive tier. Folding, the steps leave on the exit token first, then the box becomes the block; opening, the card is back at once and the steps come in with a quarter of `--travel`. The height snaps.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech:** a group named by the title. Hide (`aria-expanded="true"`, named "Hide Creating your world") and the block (`aria-expanded="false"`, named by what it shows: "Creating your world Step 1 of 3 12% Expand") are buttons that Enter and Space press. When the pressed button had focus, focus moves to the one that replaces it. The steps are a list named "Activity". One polite status outside the box says "Step 2 of 3: Populating your world", "Step 2 failed: Populating your world" and "Done: Creating your world" when they change, open or folded, and never on the first render. It does not announce each new percent.
- **Motion:** the figure's digits roll on the responsive tier; the ticks change at once and their color fades on the instant tier. In fun mode (a trial) the ring travels on the swap spring and settles a hair past its seat, and its ticks sweep to each new figure on the grow spring while the figure counts with them, never past it; at rest it is the Calm card exactly. Under reduced motion everything happens at once.
- **Touch:** the block is 44px tall and the whole of it is the target; Hide grows to 44px on a finger. A pressed block takes its hover fill.
- **Forced colors:** the block keeps a border, the lit ticks take CanvasText and the rest GrayText.

## Run list
An agent's runs for an app's sidebar or an inbox: what each run is for, where it stands, how far it has got and when it last changed. It links to a run's conversation or its Run card and replaces neither. One run as a card is a Run card; one run's steps are a Plan.
- **Header:** 32px (44px on coarse pointers): the label at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg` and the count at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, tabular ("Runs 6"), then a sm Select named "Filter runs by status" (md on coarse pointers). Its options are All and each status with its count ("Needs you 2"), and a status with no runs is disabled. A Segmented control cannot hold six options with counts in a 288px sidebar.
- **Groups:** when runs that need the person and runs that do not both show, they sit under the headings "Needs you" and "Runs" (the caption size and `--weight-label` in `--fg-subtle`, 32px); one group has no heading. The runs that need you come first, then the rest, each in the caller's order. Nothing is sorted by time, and the list reads no clock: `updated` is the caller's words ("1m ago").
- **Row:** a uniform 56px at `--radius-control`, padded `--space-2` by `--space-3`, flat. The 16px mark on the title's line, the title at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg` (a done run `--fg-muted`), cut at the end, over the status line at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`: the status word, the detail and a running run's percent ("Running · Step 2 of 5 · 40%"), cut at the end, with the `updated` words at its end, tabular, never cut. A row is a link with `href`, else a button.
- **Status:** from one table. Running is the half-filled circle in `--fg`. Needs you is the empty circle in `--fg`, and "Needs you" is the one word in `--fg` at `--weight-title`. Done is the check in `--fg-muted`. Failed is the x in `--danger` with "Failed". Stopped is the dashed circle in `--fg-subtle`. Status color stays on the mark (R5, R7).
- **Selected:** the raised block (`--block`, `--block-lift`) as a text-free backplate behind the row, and `aria-current` on the row. Hover is the Glide's one highlight moving from row to row under it, for a mouse only.
- **Verbs:** Stop on a running row and Archive on a finished one (done, failed, stopped), sm ghost icon-only buttons in a Tooltip, named with the run ("Stop Fix the Halo build"). They show on hover and focus-within, and always on coarse pointers at 44px. Every row keeps their column while the list has any, so nothing shifts when one shows. A run that needs you has none: the person answers it in the run.
- **Motion (R4):** a press glides the block to its row on the responsive tier (a shared `layoutId`). A filter press glides the rows that stay to their new places by `layout="position"` while the rows it brings fade in, on the responsive tier. A run the agent changes moves to its new place at once, the block with it, because no hand asked: the press records the filter it asked for, only the render that shows it glides, and every other render uses Motion's instant transition. Colors fade on the instant tier and a mark swaps at once.
- **Fun mode** (a trial): the block glides on the glide spring, the highlight stretches as the Glide's does, and a run that becomes done, failed or needs you pops its mark once on the pop spring. The pop's transform is cleared when it lands, so the list at rest is the Calm list exactly.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech:** the rows are one Tab stop with a roving focus (the run last focused, else the open one, else the first). ↑ and ↓ move between runs and stop at the ends, Home and End go to them, → moves to the row's verb and ← back, and Enter or Space opens the run. Each list is named by the label, or by its heading when there are two. A row is named by its title, its status line and its `updated` words ("Fix the Halo build, Needs you, Approve npm run deploy, 1m ago"). One polite status says "Fix the Halo build needs you", "Move the Northwind site failed" or "Import the Cosmos catalog is done" when a run changes into one of those, never on the first render and never for a run that only appears. When a pressed verb or Show all runs had focus and leaves, focus moves to the row that takes its place.
- **Empty:** "No runs yet." (or the caller's `empty`). A filter that shows nothing says so ("No runs need you.") with a ghost "Show all runs".
- **On compact screens and coarse pointers:** the verbs always show at 44px, the filter grows to md, a pressed row takes its hover fill, and a tap leaves no highlight behind. No swipe: Stop and Archive are buttons (a swipe to archive on the hand engine is the designed follow-up).
- **Forced colors:** the block keeps a border and takes Highlight, and the open row's words and mark take HighlightText.

## Code block
Code in an answer, in a tool's output, or a diff.
- **Surface:** `--surface-2` with an inset `--line` at `--radius-surface`. A header row 32px tall holds the file name or the language in mono at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, and a Copy button (sm ghost icon-only, "Copy code"; it swaps to a check and a polite live region says "Copied"; 44px on coarse pointers). The code is mono at the small size in `--fg`, padded `--space-4`, with a tab size of 2.
- **Highlighting:** quiet, through tokens only: identifiers and properties `--fg`, keywords and signs `--fg-muted`, comments `--fg-subtle`, strings `--accent-text` mixed 90% into `--fg`, as the gallery's own code blocks. Code is not data, so the data hues stay out (R7). JavaScript and TypeScript are highlighted; other languages show plain.
- **Diff:** with `lang="diff"`, each line's first character is its sign. The sign sits in its own column: `+` in `--success`, `-` in `--danger`. Added lines are `--fg`, removed lines `--fg-subtle`, and both changed kinds band with an fg 4% fill. Status color stays on the sign, as on an error's icon. A changed line starts with a hidden "Added:" or "Removed:" for screen readers. A diff's header lines and `@@` lines are quiet text with no sign.
- **Lines:** optional numbers in a gutter, mono at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, hidden from screen readers and from a copy.
- **Overflow:** long lines scroll sideways inside the block (or wrap with `wrap`), and a block taller than `maxHeight` scrolls down. The scroller is focusable and labelled, so a keyboard can scroll it.
- **Motion:** none. The copy icon swaps at once.

## File changes
What an agent changed in a codebase, to review before keeping it: the files touched, each with its lines added and removed, its diff and Keep and Undo, and Keep all for the rest. The same component is an edit call's details (one file) and the end of a turn (every file).
- **Surface:** bare in the turn, as a Plan is. A review is not an approval, so it is no box: the Agents rules allow a box only for the person's bubble, an approval and a code block.
- **Header** (with `summary`, the default): at least 32px tall (44px on coarse pointers). "4 files changed" at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, over a caption-size line in `--fg-subtle` at `--weight`: "15 lines added, 9 removed · 4 left to review", the count a Rolling number. At its end Keep all (a secondary sm button, the raised block) and Undo all (ghost sm). Once any file is decided they say "Keep the rest" and "Undo the rest", and both act on the pending files only, so a press never re-decides a file the person chose. With nothing pending the buttons go, the header keeps its height, and the line is a receipt: "All reviewed: 3 kept, 1 undone". Below 28rem (a container query) the buttons sit under the words.
- **The accent (R6):** Send keeps it. A review does not stop the agent, and the person may reply while reviewing, so Keep all is a raised block in a chat. `accent` makes Keep all primary on a screen with no Composer; it defaults to off, and the caller owns the one-fill rule, as with an approval.
- **Row:** a list item at least 32px tall (44px on coarse pointers) at `--radius-control`. A disclosure button holds a 16px chevron (in the column where a tool call shows its mark), a 16px FileIcon in `--fg-subtle` and the path in `--font-mono` at the small size: the directory in `--fg-subtle`, cut first and from its start so the nearest folder stays ("…styles/fonts.css"), the file name in `--fg`, never cut. After the path, at the caption size: the kind in words for added, deleted and renamed in `--fg-muted` (modified has none), the counts "+24 −6" in tabular figures (the sign in `--success` or `--danger`, the figures in `--fg-muted`, the minus U+2212), and a decided file's word. A rename's old path is a line of its own under it ("from src/styles/typography.css"). Below 28rem the kind, the counts and the word drop to a second line. A row that opens fills fg 6% under the pointer, and the fill reaches `--space-2` past the words on each side.
- **Decided:** "Kept" with the check in `--fg-muted`, or "Undone" with the undo mark in `--fg-subtle`, and the file name steps back to the same ink. No strikethrough and no tinted rows: color stays on the signs (R7), the word says the state (R5).
- **Verbs:** beside the button, outside it, Keep (the check) and Undo (the undo mark), sm ghost icon-only buttons in Tooltips, named with the file ("Keep src/app/layout.tsx"), 44px on coarse pointers. A row shows the verbs that differ from its state: pending shows both, kept shows Undo, undone shows Keep. Each verb has a fixed slot, so a verb never moves and nothing beside a decided row shifts. No single-letter keys.
- **Diff:** a Code block with `lang="diff"`, no file name (the row says it) and a 20rem cap, indented `--space-6` under the row. One change starts open, many start closed, and `defaultOpen` overrides. A change with no diff has no chevron, unless the caller passes `onOpenChange`: then it opens and says "Loading the diff" in `--fg-subtle` until the caller passes one. No spinner, no dots.
- **Counts:** `added` and `removed` when given, else counted from the diff's hunks as Code block reads them (`+` and `-` lines, never the `+++` and `---` header lines).
- **Decisions:** `onDecide(ids, decision)` is the only write path, and the component holds no decision: each change's `state` (pending, kept or undone) is the caller's. Keep all passes every pending id, so pressing it twice converges. `FileChange` is a union on `kind`: only a rename carries `from`, and nobody can decide "pending".
- **Keyboard and assistive tech:** the files are a list named by `label` ("Files changed"). Tab visits each row's disclosure (`aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` while open), then its verbs; Enter or Space opens the diff, whose scroller is the next stop. A row is named in one run: "src/styles/type.css, renamed from src/styles/typography.css, 1 line added, 1 removed, kept"; the drawn parts are hidden. One polite status says the decisions made with the list's own buttons once the caller has set them: "Kept src/app/layout.tsx", "Undid 3 files" and "All reviewed: 3 kept, 1 undone", never on the first render. A second copy of the same changes (an edit call's beside the review) shows a decision made in the other without saying it again. When the verb pressed had focus and goes, focus moves to the verb that replaces it; when Keep all and Undo all go, to the header, which reads the receipt.
- **Motion:** a decision settles the row: the verbs give way to the word at once, the file name steps back on the instant tier, and the header's count rolls down on the responsive tier. The chevron turns a quarter on the instant tier. A diff fades in and travels a quarter of `--travel` on the responsive tier and leaves on the exit token; its height snaps. In fun mode (a trial) the check of each row Keep all decides pops in a wave down the list (scale 0.6 to 1 on the pop spring, a wave step apart), and a single Keep pops its own; only a press pops a check, and at rest it is the Calm list exactly. Under reduced motion everything happens at once.
- **Read-only:** with no `onDecide` there are no verbs and no header buttons, and the rows show their state words only. **Empty:** "No files changed." in `--fg-subtle`.
- **Forced colors:** the signs and marks are glyphs, the state is words, and Keep all keeps the Button's border.

# Commerce

A store: a product, a collection and a cart. The parts know nothing about where the store lives. They take plain data (an amount and a currency code, a product, a cart line), so a Shopify store, a Stripe store and a file of products use the same parts; the `shopify` registry item turns Shopify's Storefront API into that data. The rules are the ones that hold everywhere: one accent fill per view (Add to cart on a product, Check out in the cart), a price ranks by weight and ink under the one 18px title, never by size or red, and nothing performs. There is no checkout part: Shopify's checkout is its own hosted page, so litt's part ends at Check out. The decisions are in `proposals/commerce.md`.

## Price
One amount, with what it was and what it costs per unit when those matter.
- **Type:** the amount at the size of where it sits, `--weight-title`, `--fg`, in the sans with tabular figures, as a Stat's value. Formatted by `Intl.NumberFormat` from the amount and its currency code (Shopify's `MoneyV2`), in the page's locale. Whole amounts drop the cents ("€90"); any other keeps two places ("€12.50").
- **From:** a product whose variants differ in price shows its lowest after "From" in `--fg-subtle` at `--weight`.
- **Sale:** a compare-at price higher than the price follows it, struck through, in `--fg-subtle` at `--weight`. Hidden words say "Sale price" and "Regular price", so the strike is never the only signal. `saving` adds a neutral Tag after it ("Save 25%"). Never red, never the accent, never a status color: a sale is not a status.
- **Unit price:** an optional line under it at the caption size in `--fg-subtle` ("€4.20 / 100 ml").
- **Motion:** none. A price that changes just changes; a total that changes while someone watches is a Rolling number.

## Option picker
One option of a product, such as its size or its color, as a choice of its values.
- **Anatomy:** a fieldset of native radios. The legend is visible: the option's name at the small size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, then the picked value's name in `--fg-subtle` at `--weight` ("Frame · Natural oak"), so a swatch's name is always written somewhere.
- **Text values:** the Segmented control's look and motion: one sunken tray, equal segments, the picked one the raised block (R3). The track fills the width of its column, so options of one product line up.
- **Swatch values:** a value with a color or an image is a chip, 24px (32 on coarse pointers), at `--radius-control` with an fg-12% hairline, `--space-2` apart, as the Color picker's swatches. The color is the product's, not litt's: content, like a photograph, never a data hue and never chrome (R7 does not apply). It fills only the chip. The picked chip carries a 16px check in black or white, whichever reads better on that color by APCA, as the Color picker's ink. An image swatch shows the image under the same hairline.
- **Sold out:** a value with no variant in stock among the other picks stays pickable. Its label (or chip) steps back to `--fg-disabled` (a chip to 40% opacity under its hairline), it says "sold out" in hidden text, and when it is picked the legend says "Sold out" after its name. No slash across a swatch, no strike: the words carry it, and Add to cart becomes a disabled "Sold out" button.
- **Keyboard:** the radios' own: ← → between values, Tab to the next option.
- **Motion:** the text track's surface glides on the responsive tier, as the Segmented control's. Swatches do not move; the check appears at once.

## Product card
One product in a grid, which opens its page.
- **Anatomy:** a Card with an inset cover (the Card's rules: `.litt-raised`, the fill steps one on hover, nothing lifts). The cover is a Media at 4/5 by default, the control radius, the product's first image or drawn art. Under it, the title at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, an optional vendor above it at the caption size in `--fg-subtle`, and the Price under the title (From when the variants differ).
- **One link:** the title is the card's link (CardLink), stretched over the card. A product card has no buy button and no accent: a grid of them would be a dozen accent fills (R6), and the card's job is to open the product.
- **Sold out:** a product with nothing in stock keeps its card and adds a neutral "Sold out" Tag after the price.
- **Grid:** two across on a phone, then as many 14rem columns as fit, `--space-4` apart, in a `ul`. Every cover shares one shape, so the rows stay even.
- **Never** a second picture on hover, a badge over the picture, or a picture that zooms.

## Cart
The cart is a sheet: the Dialog at `side="right"`, a bottom sheet on compact screens, so it sits above the keyboard and drags down to close.
- **Cart line:** a 64px Media at 1/1 and the control radius, the title at the body size and `--weight-title` in `--fg`, the picked options under it at the small size in `--fg-subtle` ("A2 · Natural oak"), a Number field at sm for the quantity (its label hidden, "Quantity of Open water" for screen readers), Remove as an sm ghost button, and the line's price at the end. Lines are a `ul`, split by `--line` hairlines, `--space-4` above and below.
- **Remove:** a line goes at once, with Undo in a toast ("Removed Open water"). No confirm: it can be undone (Slide to confirm, the rule).
- **Summary:** pinned at the sheet's foot: the subtotal as one labelled row, "Subtotal" in `--fg-muted` and its figure at the end at `--weight-title`, a Rolling number; an optional free-shipping Meter in `--fg`, never a hue ("€12 to free shipping", "Free shipping"); "Taxes and shipping at checkout" at the small size in `--fg-subtle`; and Check out, a primary md button the width of the sheet: the open cart's one accent fill. Check out goes to the store's checkout (Shopify's `checkoutUrl`).
- **Empty:** the Empty state: "Your cart is empty" and one secondary action back to the products. No Check out.
- **Cart button:** the header's way in, a ghost button with the bag icon and the count in tabular figures, named "Cart, 3 items". The count is a Rolling number, so an add is seen where the cart lives.
- **After Add to cart:** the button says "Added" with a check for 2s (instant tier), the header's count rolls, and a toast says "Added Open water" with View cart. The cart does not open by itself (`onAdd="open"` opens it instead). No picture flies to the cart: motion confirms, it never performs.
- **Motion:** the sheet's own. Lines leave at once; a quantity changes at once; the subtotal rolls.
