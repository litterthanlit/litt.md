Give those stages the plate and the recorder's copy, and widen `test:materials` to read them: it reads only the lead stage and Variants today, which is why it passes.
3. **The dial says what its edges do (finding 6).** Previous, Next, Index and Every component are 10 to 12px glyphs. Add a word that shows on hover and on focus (a tooltip in Device's own LCD chip), and keep the names a screen reader already gets. At 1366 by 768 the dial falls below the fold: shorten the screen at that height so the whole recorder is in view.
4. **The Mute key has a word on a phone (finding 8, part).**
